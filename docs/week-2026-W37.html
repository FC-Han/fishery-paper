水产养殖应对环境胁迫与种质资源保护研究进展

本周研究概览

本周共收录6篇渔业相关论文，研究方向涵盖水产病害防控、鱼类生理生态、养殖模式创新、饲料安全及副产物高值化利用等。整体来看，研究呈现出明显的应用导向特征：一方面聚焦水产养殖的可持续发展与食品安全问题（如疫苗研发、生物絮团养殖、霉菌毒素污染评估），另一方面关注环境变化与养殖管理对鱼类健康及产品质量的影响（如温度与密度对汞积累的作用）。值得注意的是，本周研究在方法上体现了从传统养殖技术向分子生物学、生态能量学及循环经济理念融合的多元化趋势。

创新性研究

  1. 《The Vaccine Candidates Against Major Fish Pathogens (Pseudomonas fluorescens and Aeromonas hydrophila) from the Outer Membrane Proteins of P. fluorescens in Fish》 — 该研究系统克隆并纯化了24种荧光假单胞菌外膜蛋白（OMPs），并制备了16种小鼠抗血清，探索了跨物种疫苗开发的可行性。其创新之处在于利用一种病原菌的OMPs同时应对两种主要鱼类病原菌的感染威胁，为水产养殖中广谱疫苗的研发提供了新思路，有望降低疫苗开发成本并提高防控效率。
  1. 《Ecological energetics of an imperilled fish: Physiological metrics, experimental protocols and implications for population recovery》 — 针对濒危鱼类生理数据采集困难的瓶颈，该研究系统评估了多种生态能量学指标的适用性，并提出了标准化的实验方案。其创新性在于将生理学指标与种群恢复管理直接对接，为濒危鱼类的保护性养殖和野外放归提供了可操作的量化工具，填补了该领域方法学空白。
  1. 《Biofloc-based aquaculture: From monoculture to integrated systems》 — 该综述突破了传统生物絮团技术（BFT）仅用于单一养殖模式的认知框架，系统梳理了其向综合养殖系统（如鱼菜共生、多营养级综合养殖）拓展的路径与潜力。其创新性在于从系统整合的视角重新审视BFT的技术价值，为水产养殖的绿色转型提供了新的理论框架和实践方向。
  1. 《Global Occurrence, Co-Contamination Patterns, and Toxic Effects of Mycotoxins in Fish Feed: A Multi-Species Perspective Review》 — 该研究首次从多鱼种视角系统分析了全球鱼饲料中霉菌毒素的发生率、共污染模式及毒性效应。其创新之处在于揭示了多种霉菌毒素共存时的协同或拮抗效应，突破了以往单一毒素风险评估的局限，对饲料安全标准的制定具有重要的参考价值。

Stable Isotope Analyses of Yellowfin and Blackfin Tuna Eye Lenses Reveal Life History Patterns
👤 Rullo, Kylee M.; Peebles, Ernst B.; Vecchio, Julie L.; Muraw... 📅 2026-09-12
ABSTRACT Spatial geography, ontogenetic movement, and trophic patterns of mobile species are key elements of species life history, and knowledge of them is critical for effective marine resource management. For yellowfin tuna ( Thunnus albacares ; YFT) and blackfin tuna ( Thunnus atlanticus ; BLFT), residency durations and ontogenetic movements in the Gulf of Mexico (Gulf) are uncertain. To evaluate changes in trophic position and movement of YFT ( n = 46) and BLFT ( n = 31) caught in the northern Gulf, we used nitrogen (δ 15 N) and carbon (δ 13 C) stable isotope analysis across eye lenses. Lifetime increases in isotopic values, significant correlations between each isotopic value and laminar midpoint (proxy for length; LM), and a significant correlation between δ 15 N and δ 13 C values would indicate fewer movements across isotopic gradients and the potential for long‐term residency. In this study, all individuals increased in δ 15 N from core to outermost lamina (YFT mean = 4.47‰, BLFT mean = 5.06‰), and 95% of individuals had significant, positive correlations between δ 15 N and LM, consistent with increasing trophic position and/or migration from lower δ 15 N areas to the northern Gulf. Intraspecific smoothed curves of δ 13 C by size exhibited early dips in δ 13 C values and subsequent increases around estimated age 1, revealing possible ontogenetic or seasonal movement by both species. This study was limited in giving a definitive interpretation of movement, but some individual profiles (YFT n = 15, BLFT n = 2) displayed significant, positive correlations between δ 13 C and δ 15 N, signaling a trophic position increase with potential long‐term residencies.

Abstract Recent oligotrophication and rising water temperatures in the Seto Inland Sea of Japan have led to a decline in oyster production in Hiroshima Prefecture. This study developed and evaluated the “Seawater Pump-up Apparatus for Localized Oceanic Warming-mitigation” (SPALOW), a device designed to supply cool and nutrient-rich bottom water and diatom resting cells from the sediment to the surface layer. Geometric morphometric analyses of intragenus (Campylomormyrus) and intergenus hybrids in mormyrid fish
👤 Amen, Rahma; Berghäuser, Timo; Kirschbaum, Frank; Tiedemann,... 📅 2026-09-12
Abstract Natural hybrids within the weakly electric mormyrid fish genus Campylomormyrus have not been documented in the wild. However, recent advances have enabled the artificial production of both intragenus ( Campylomormyrus ) and intergenus ( Campylomormyrus and Gnathonemus ) hybrids. While previous studies have focused mainly on phenotypic divergence in body shape, especially electric organ discharges, ecophenotypical divergence in these hybrids remains less explored. To address this gap, we used geometric morphometrics to analyze body shape variation in intragenus and intergenus hybrids relative to their parental species. Using standardized digital photographs, we examined hybrids derived from parental species with varying genetic and phenotypic distances, including distinct snout morphologies. Our results show that hybrids are morphologically distinguishable but generally remain within the phenotypic range of their parental species. In most crosses, hybrids exhibited intermediate morphology or stronger similarity to one parent, whereas transgressive shape segregation was observed only in one cross and did not involve snout length. Snout thickness and body curvature showed more variable patterns among crosses than snout length. These findings suggest that F1 hybrid morphology is shaped mainly by intermediate expression with cross-specific parental affinity, while strongly novel morphologies appear to be rare. This study improves our understanding of hybridization in Mormyridae, its potential relevance for phenotypic diversification, and provides a basis for future work on later hybrid generations.

A Study on Monoculture of Indigenous Fresh Water Fish Channa striatus in a Fresh Water Pond in Tamil Nadu, India
👤 Sivakami, R.; Nazar, A. R.; Babu, V.; Rajendiran, A. 📅 2026-09-12
Striped murrel (Channa striatus) is an indigenous freshwater fish of economic and nutritional importance in Tamil Nadu, India. The present study evaluated its monoculture in a perennial freshwater pond at Swamimalai, Kumbakonam Taluk, Tanjore District, over a five-month period from February to July 2026. A total of 1,200 fingerlings with an initial total weight of 7.8 kg were stocked. Natural feed comprising chicken waste, fish waste, and live Gambusia fish was provided together with artificial floating and immersed feeds. Feeding was maintained at 2% of total body weight, and fish growth was assessed at 15-day intervals. At harvest, 986 fish were recovered, corresponding to a survival rate of 82.16%. The harvested fish had a total weight of 436.255 kg, with individual weights ranging from 368.4 to 516.5 g and an average weight of 442.45 g. Fish were marketed according to size at rates ranging from Rs. 320 to Rs. 450 per kg. The total income from the culture operation was Rs. 1,79,063.20, while total expenditure was Rs. 73,650.00, resulting in a net profit of Rs. 1,05,413.20 over five months. The findings indicate that pond-based monoculture of Channa striatus, when supported by appropriate stocking, feeding, and culture management, can provide a productive and economically favourable freshwater aquaculture option under the conditions examined in Tamil Nadu.

Fish Diversity and Its Relation to Water Quality Parameters of Markonahalli Reservoir, Tumkur District, Karnataka, India
👤 S. R., Chalapathi; H. M., Lalitha 📅 2026-09-12
Markonahalli Reservoir in Tumkur District, Karnataka, is used mainly for irrigation and fishing. This study assessed fish diversity and selected physicochemical water-quality parameters from February 2024 to January 2025 to examine reservoir conditions in relation to the recorded fish community. Fish were collected with the assistance of local fishermen using nets of different sizes and types, while water samples were collected monthly from four sampling sites. Water temperature, pH, turbidity, electrical conductivity, nitrate, sulphate, dissolved oxygen, and biological oxygen demand were analysed using standard methods. A total of 16 fish species representing 14 genera, seven families, and five orders were recorded, with Cypriniformes forming the dominant taxonomic group. The measured parameters showed spatial and seasonal variation across the sampling sites. Turbidity ranged from 2.75±0.418 to 6.25±0.7275 NTU, while electrical conductivity ranged from 403±11.358 to 856.33±14.263 µS/cm. Nitrate concentrations ranged from 2.425±0.025 to 4.825±0.307 mg/L, and sulphate ranged from 5±2.00 to 34±2.122 mg/L. Dissolved oxygen varied from 4.85±0.593 to 6.5333±0.177 mg/L, whereas biological oxygen demand ranged from 2.4667±0.134 to 4.4±0.548 mg/L. The findings provide baseline information on fish diversity and seasonal water-quality conditions in Markonahalli Reservoir and support continued monitoring for the sustainable management of its fishery and aquatic resources over time.
