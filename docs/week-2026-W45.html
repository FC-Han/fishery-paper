SIMULATION AND FINANCIAL FEASIBILITY OF SEINE NET FISHERIES AT BRONDONG FISHING PORT
👤 Putrama, Abyan; Bangun, Tri Nanda Citra; Kurniawati, Vita Ru... 📅 2026-11-06
The transition from cantrang to seine net (Jaring Tarik Berkantong/JTB) has raised fishers’ concerns regarding potential declines in catch production and operational profitability. This study was conducted to analyze operational costs, cost-determining factors, and profit decline simulations as a basis for assessing the sustainability of fishing gear transition. The objectives of this study were to estimate fishing operational costs, identify factors influencing operational costs, and analyze the tolerance threshold of profit decline under seine net operations. This research employed a descriptive case study approach through observation and structured interviews involving 15 respondents, consisting of vessel owners and skippers selected using purposive sampling. Data were analyzed using Causal Loop Diagram (CLD), Revenue-Cost Ratio (R/C Ratio), Break Even Point (BEP), and catch decline simulation scenarios. The results showed that average operational costs reached IDR 46.1 million per trip, with fuel accounting for the largest proportion (54%). The R/C Ratio of 2.08 indicated that the business was financially feasible, with a production break-even point of 3,523.56 kg per trip. Simulation results revealed that the business remained viable under catch reductions of up to 40%, whereas declines exceeding 55% could potentially lead to financial losses. These findings indicate that the transition to seine net remains economically promising, provided that operational efficiency is strengthened as an adaptive strategy for fishers. Key words: cantrang, seine net, financial feasibility, operational-costs, profit-simulation

Eating with a grain of salt: Trade-offs between habitat use and individual foraging specialisation drive growth across ecotypes of a freshwater piscivore (pike, Esox lucius) inhabiting brackish habitats
👤 Rittweg, Timo Dustin; Trueman, Clive; Goldhammer, Tobias; Br... 📅 2026-11-06
Freshwater fishes living in brackish environments often diverge into ecotypes displaying different habitat use. Salinity levels common to brackish habitats incur osmoregulatory costs to freshwater fishes, but also offer opportunities in the form of access to marine prey, creating trade-offs. We investigated foraging specialisation of coastal northern pike (Esox lucius) ecotypes, ranging from migratory to brackish- or freshwater- residents, sampled from coastal lagoons and freshwater tributaries in the southern Baltic Sea. We combined resource use with individual growth as a fitness surrogate to identify mechanisms of coexistence. Individual foraging specialisation was highest in behavioural phenotypes with high trophic overlap in shared foraging grounds, brackish resident pike and cross-habitat pike. Larger brackish-adapted individuals specialised more on marine resources with positive effects on growth, but higher-than average salinities reduced growth. Trophic overlap of anadromous and freshwater pike phenotypes was low and differed from cross-habitat and brackish habitat phenotypes. Age-specific effects of marine resources and salinity on individual growth suggest coexistence of pike ecotypes is facilitated by dynamic trade-offs between foraging specialisation and habitat costs.

Estimating divergence and migration times of sticklebacks (Gasterosteidae): a fossil-based analysis using the Adaptive Beta method
👤 Frank, Tanner Matthew; Marshall, Charles R.; Bell, Michael A... 📅 2026-11-07
The timing of major events in stickleback evolution is important for determining rates of molecular evolution within the group. Inferred substitution rates depend on fossil-calibrated divergence times, but in sticklebacks these currently rest on the single oldest fossil occurrence or calibrations largely from other fish taxa, leaving them vulnerable to the idiosyncrasies of the fossil record. We infer a chronology for major events in stickleback evolution using the Adaptive Beta method (ABM) on subsets of a record of 53 gasterosteid fossil occurrences. By incorporating multiple fossil ages in a Bayesian framework, this approach produces credible intervals that account for varying fossil recovery potential through time. Our analysis indicates that the Gasterosteidae originated approximately 32.9 Mya (95% CI: 24.5-54.3 Mya), with the divergence between Gasterosteus and other genera, including Pungitius, occurring at approximately 20.8 Mya (95% CI: 13.8-40.7 Mya). Furthermore, the results inform the timing of inter-oceanic dispersal: we find that Gasterosteus aculeatus Linnaeus, 1758 likely entered the Atlantic only 0.9 Mya (95% CI: 0.6-1.7 Mya). Inclusion of uncertain early Atlantic basin fossils pushes this invasion back to 6.4 Mya (95% CI: 4.8-10.4 Mya). These interval-based estimates provide a reliable chronological framework for calibrating rates of nucleotide substitution in sticklebacks.

DNA barcoding of freshwater fish species from Upper Lake: A Ramsar site, Bhopal, India: Implications for species authentication and reference barcode database development
👤 Uikey, N.; Yadav, S.; Garg, R. K. 📅 2026-11-06
Upper Lake, Bhopal, a Ramsar designated wetland and one of the largest natural freshwater lakes in central India, supports rich fish diversity and provides important ecological, economic, and fisheries resources. Accurate identification is expected to make proper conservation of any animal species, yet traditional morphometric methods are challenging and time consuming. To address these limitations, this study employed DNA barcoding using the mitochondrial cytochrome-c oxidase subunit I (mtcox1) gene to generate DNA barcodes for 24 fish specimens representing 20 species from 7 orders collected from Upper Lake, Bhopal. We generated sequences ranging from 560 - 640 bp and deposited them in GenBank and the Barcode of Life Data System (BOLD Systems). The mean intraspecific divergence was extremely low (0.58%), exhibiting a significant separation as a clear barcode gap from the minimum interspecific divergence at the genus level (10.24%). Nearest-neighbor method supported species delimitation with a mean interspecific distance (14.7%) 122.5- fold greater than the mean intraspecific distance (0.12%). However, the Barcode Index Number (BIN) analysis revealed notable instances of taxonomic discordance with 24 sequences generating 18 BINs. Nucleotide composition analysis showed a mean GC content of 45.38% with a characteristic positional bias, highest at the first codon position (mean 56.25%) and lowest at the third (37.32%). This study established a novel genetic reference dataset for the fish fauna of this Ramsar designated wetland and demonstrated the utility of the mtcox1 gene for species identification, biodiversity monitoring, and conservation management of freshwater ecosystems.

Graphene oxide (GO) effectively adsorbs heavy metal ions but can be toxic to fish. A composite of PVA hydrogel with GO enhances water quality maintenance in aquaculture. While PVA-GO hydrogel's adsorption capacity has been studied, its environmental and toxicological impacts are unclear. This study examines the biocompatibility of low-concentration PVA-GO hydrogel composites for water treatment, comparing untreated, metal-contaminated, and treated water samples in Oreochromis niloticus aquaculture. Additionally, GO and PVA-GO hydrogels exhibited intestinal antibacterial activity. This research suggests that combining GO with PVA hydrogel reduces toxicity to fish, improves heavy metal removal, and enhances their health in aquaculture environments.

👤 Mahendra, Diana; Wahyuningsih, Sri 📅 2026-11-06
This study seeks to analyze the needs of Fisheries and Marine Science students in learning English to be a basis in developing a contextually relevant English for Specific Purposes (ESP) curriculum. The study used a descriptive qualitative design and included 23 participants: 20 undergraduate students in the third to fifth semesters, two English lecturers, and one Marine Science lecturer. Data were collected using semi-structured interviews, focus group discussions, classroom observations and document analysis and analysed thematically using the concepts of necessities, lacks and wants. The results of the study suggest the need for an ESP curriculum that incorporates academic literacy, subject specific vocabulary and practical communication activities. The study offers an empirical foundation for synchronizing English teaching to the academic requirements and anticipated professional communication skills of Fisheries and Marine Science students.

Assessing the adaptation between recreational fisheries development and aquaculture efficiency in multifunctional freshwater fisheries systems: Evidence from China
👤 Hu, Xianglan; Liu, Jingzhou 📅 2026-11-06
To examine the quality of synergy and potential mismatch risks between production and recreational functions during the multifunctional transformation of freshwater fisheries, this study constructs a composite index of recreational fisheries development using panel data from 29 Chinese provinces from 2008 to 2023. Freshwater aquaculture efficiency is measured using a benevolent cross-efficiency model incorporating both desirable and undesirable outputs. An integrated adaptation model is then employed to capture the spatiotemporal differentiation of the adaptation relationship between the two systems, while Random Forest and SHAP methods are applied to identify nonlinear effects and threshold characteristics of key drivers. The results indicate that: (1) both recreational fisheries development and aquaculture efficiency increased steadily over the study period, whereas the composite adaptation index remained at low-to-medium levels with limited stage-wise improvement, suggesting that efficiency gains have not been effectively translated into recreational advantages; (2) the adaptation pattern exhibits pronounced regional differentiation and path dependence, characterized by persistently higher levels in central China, gradual improvement in eastern China, increasing polarization in western China, and consistently low and weakening adaptation in northeastern China; and (3) the driving mechanisms are markedly nonlinear, with water environmental pressure and tourism reception intensity acting as key constraints. Specifically, water quality pressure exhibits an inverted U-shaped effect with clear thresholds, whereas tourism intensity exerts a sustained inhibitory effect. 👤 Bertram, Alice; Schatz, Valentin 📅 2026-11-07
Abstract This case note analyses the EU General Court's judgment in VDK v Commission (Judgment of 21 May 2025, Case T‐265/23), which concerns the legality of spatial fisheries restrictions adopted to protect marine Natura 2000 sites in the German Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ). As the applicant did not appeal the judgment, it has become final. The applicant, a German fishers' association, challenged the Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/340, which closed four sites in Germany's North Sea EEZ (in addition to three Dutch sites) to bottom‐contacting gear and to gillnets and entangling nets. The applicant argued that the measure was, insofar as it concerned the German sites, disproportionate and that the European Commission exceeded its powers under the Common Fisheries Policy (CFP). The Court dismissed both pleas. First, it reaffirmed the broad discretion enjoyed by European Union (EU) institutions in the field of the CFP, applying a deferential standard of judicial review and endorsing a precautionary approach to bottom‐contacting fishing gear in marine protected areas. Second, it confirmed the Commission's competence to protect gravel beds under Article 11(2) CFP‐Regulation in conjunction with Article 13(4) MSFD. The judgment strengthens the legal resilience of spatial fisheries restrictions used to implement EU biodiversity law, such as the Birds and Habitats Directives. However, consistent with past judgments, this limited standard of judicial review will generally shield measures adopted under the CFP from challenge, specifically when they are challenged based on allegedly too high environmental protection ambition.
