创新性研究

  1. Effects of waterborne exposure to crude mulberry leaf polysaccharides on lipopolysaccharide-induced acute inflammatory responses in gibel carp (*Carassius gibelio*): insights into host immune and intestinal responses（*Aquaculture*）

该研究的创新点在于采用水体暴露（waterborne exposure）途径递送桑叶粗多糖，而非传统的饲料添加方式，并同时考察宿主免疫与肠道响应两个层面。若结果证实水体暴露可有效调控LPS诱导的急性炎症，将为水产养殖中免疫增强剂的给药方式提供低成本、低应激的替代方案，对集约化养殖中的炎症性疾病管理具有潜在应用价值。

  1. Comparative analysis of immune responses reveals mechanisms underlying different susceptibility to *Cryptocaryon irritans* in large yellow croaker (*Larimichthys crocea*) and red sea bream (*Pagrus major*)（*Aquaculture*）

该研究以两种经济海水鱼为对象，通过比较免疫应答解析对刺激隐核虫易感性差异的机制，创新性体现在以“同病原、异宿主”的比较免疫学框架定位种间抗性差异的关键环节。研究结果有望为抗病育种标记筛选和物种混养策略提供理论依据，对海水网箱养殖中白点病的防控具有直接参考意义。

  1. Comparison of EBER flow FISH results from two facilities（*Cytometry Part B: Clinical Cytometry*）

该文虽属临床病毒学范畴，但其方法学核心——EBER flow FISH的跨机构可比性验证——对渔业免疫学中流式细胞术方法的标准化具有借鉴意义。多中心方法一致性评估的思路可迁移至鱼类免疫细胞表型分析的质量控制，提示本领域在方法学报告规范上仍有提升空间。

Comparison of EBER flow FISH results from two facilities
👤 Nishimura, Madoka; Tomomasa, Dan; Watanabe, Eri; Tsuchiya, K... 📅 2026-11-02
Abstract Epstein–Barr virus (EBV) primarily infects B cells but can occasionally target T and/or NK cells, leading to chronic active EBV disease or EBV‐associated hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis, which are often difficult to treat. EBV‐associated diseases exhibit a wide range of phenotypes. EBV‐infected cells play a major role in pathogenesis, making their identification essential for diagnosis. EBV‐encoded small RNA (EBER) flow fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH), combined with multicolor flow cytometry, allows highly sensitive detection of EBV‐infected cells, even from limited samples. However, EBER flow FISH is labor‐intensive and requires technical expertise, limiting its use in standard facilities. We investigated whether the technique used in our department could be successfully implemented in other facilities. We performed EBER flow FISH at our department (Facility A) on cryopreserved peripheral blood mononuclear cell samples from seven patients diagnosed with EBV‐associated diseases. The same assay was then conducted at Facility B, and results were compared for cell populations and the frequency of EBER‐positive cells within each population. Significant correlations were observed between facilities for both cell fraction proportions and the proportion of EBER‐positive cells. Among data points where both facilities detected ≥5% EBER‐positive cells, 6 of 8 data points (75%) were within the acceptance interval of ±20%. Although one patient with very low proportions of EBER‐positive cells showed inconsistent findings, overall results did not differ significantly between facilities. Although further validation in larger multicenter studies is required, these findings suggest preliminary interlaboratory comparability and support the potential transferability of EBER flow FISH across institutions.
