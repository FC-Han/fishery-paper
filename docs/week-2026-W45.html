创新性研究

  1. Effects of waterborne exposure to crude mulberry leaf polysaccharides on lipopolysaccharide-induced acute inflammatory responses in gibel carp (*Carassius gibelio*): insights into host immune and intestinal responses（*Aquaculture*）

该研究的创新点在于采用水体暴露（waterborne exposure）途径递送桑叶粗多糖，而非传统的饲料添加方式，并同时考察宿主免疫与肠道响应两个层面。若结果证实水体暴露可有效调控LPS诱导的急性炎症，将为水产养殖中免疫增强剂的给药方式提供低成本、低应激的替代方案，对集约化养殖中的炎症性疾病管理具有潜在应用价值。

  1. Comparative analysis of immune responses reveals mechanisms underlying different susceptibility to *Cryptocaryon irritans* in large yellow croaker (*Larimichthys crocea*) and red sea bream (*Pagrus major*)（*Aquaculture*）

该研究以两种经济海水鱼为对象，通过比较免疫应答解析对刺激隐核虫易感性差异的机制，创新性体现在以“同病原、异宿主”的比较免疫学框架定位种间抗性差异的关键环节。研究结果有望为抗病育种标记筛选和物种混养策略提供理论依据，对海水网箱养殖中白点病的防控具有直接参考意义。

  1. Comparison of EBER flow FISH results from two facilities（*Cytometry Part B: Clinical Cytometry*）

该文虽属临床病毒学范畴，但其方法学核心——EBER flow FISH的跨机构可比性验证——对渔业免疫学中流式细胞术方法的标准化具有借鉴意义。多中心方法一致性评估的思路可迁移至鱼类免疫细胞表型分析的质量控制，提示本领域在方法学报告规范上仍有提升空间。

Estimating divergence and migration times of sticklebacks (Gasterosteidae): a fossil-based analysis using the Adaptive Beta method
👤 Frank, Tanner Matthew; Marshall, Charles R.; Bell, Michael A... 📅 2026-11-07
The timing of major events in stickleback evolution is important for determining rates of molecular evolution within the group. Inferred substitution rates depend on fossil-calibrated divergence times, but in sticklebacks these currently rest on the single oldest fossil occurrence or calibrations largely from other fish taxa, leaving them vulnerable to the idiosyncrasies of the fossil record. We infer a chronology for major events in stickleback evolution using the Adaptive Beta method (ABM) on subsets of a record of 53 gasterosteid fossil occurrences. By incorporating multiple fossil ages in a Bayesian framework, this approach produces credible intervals that account for varying fossil recovery potential through time. Our analysis indicates that the Gasterosteidae originated approximately 32.9 Mya (95% CI: 24.5-54.3 Mya), with the divergence between Gasterosteus and other genera, including Pungitius, occurring at approximately 20.8 Mya (95% CI: 13.8-40.7 Mya). Furthermore, the results inform the timing of inter-oceanic dispersal: we find that Gasterosteus aculeatus Linnaeus, 1758 likely entered the Atlantic only 0.9 Mya (95% CI: 0.6-1.7 Mya). Inclusion of uncertain early Atlantic basin fossils pushes this invasion back to 6.4 Mya (95% CI: 4.8-10.4 Mya). These interval-based estimates provide a reliable chronological framework for calibrating rates of nucleotide substitution in sticklebacks.

Physiological and histological study of the toxicity and biocompatibility of graphene oxide and GO–PVA nanocomposite hydrogels in the treatment of metal-contaminated water for Oreochromis niloticus aquaculture
👤 Moustafa, Basma H.; Serag, Eman; Zeid, Salma M. S. 📅 2026-11-07
Abstract Heavy metals in aquaculture water from industrial effluent, mining, and agricultural runoff pose risks to ecosystems and human health. Graphene oxide (GO) effectively adsorbs heavy metal ions but can be toxic to fish. A composite of PVA hydrogel with GO enhances water quality maintenance in aquaculture. While PVA-GO hydrogel's adsorption capacity has been studied, its environmental and toxicological impacts are unclear. This study examines the biocompatibility of low-concentration PVA-GO hydrogel composites for water treatment, comparing untreated, metal-contaminated, and treated water samples in Oreochromis niloticus aquaculture. The synthesis of GO and PVA-GO hydrogel was analyzed using X-ray diffraction (XRD), scanning electron microscopy (SEM), and FTIR spectra. The results indicated that heavy metal-contaminated water caused significant anemia and altered plasma biochemistry, while the biomarkers in treated groups returned to levels similar to the control. Additionally, GO and PVA-GO hydrogels exhibited intestinal antibacterial activity. This research suggests that combining GO with PVA hydrogel reduces toxicity to fish, improves heavy metal removal, and enhances their health in aquaculture environments.

Judicial review of bottom‐trawling restrictions in EU marine protected areas: Case T‐265/23 VDK v Commission
👤 Bertram, Alice; Schatz, Valentin 📅 2026-11-07
Abstract This case note analyses the EU General Court's judgment in VDK v Commission (Judgment of 21 May 2025, Case T‐265/23), which concerns the legality of spatial fisheries restrictions adopted to protect marine Natura 2000 sites in the German Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ). As the applicant did not appeal the judgment, it has become final. The applicant, a German fishers' association, challenged the Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/340, which closed four sites in Germany's North Sea EEZ (in addition to three Dutch sites) to bottom‐contacting gear and to gillnets and entangling nets. The applicant argued that the measure was, insofar as it concerned the German sites, disproportionate and that the European Commission exceeded its powers under the Common Fisheries Policy (CFP). The Court dismissed both pleas. First, it reaffirmed the broad discretion enjoyed by European Union (EU) institutions in the field of the CFP, applying a deferential standard of judicial review and endorsing a precautionary approach to bottom‐contacting fishing gear in marine protected areas. Second, it confirmed the Commission's competence to protect gravel beds under Article 11(2) CFP‐Regulation in conjunction with Article 13(4) MSFD. The judgment strengthens the legal resilience of spatial fisheries restrictions used to implement EU biodiversity law, such as the Birds and Habitats Directives. However, consistent with past judgments, this limited standard of judicial review will generally shield measures adopted under the CFP from challenge, specifically when they are challenged based on allegedly too high environmental protection ambition.

Food Security in the Sultanate of Oman, 1975–2025: Fisheries, Falaj Agriculture and the Rise of Diversified Self-Sufficiency
👤 Nosir, Walid 📅 2026-11-08
Oman differs from its Gulf neighbours in several respects relevant to food security: a long Arabian Sea coastline with productive fisheries, a monsoon-influenced southern region (Dhofar), an ancient irrigation tradition based on the falaj system, and major ports at Sohar, Duqm and Salalah that lie outside, or at the mouth of, the Strait of Hormuz. Since the start of the modern state in 1970, its population has grown from well under one million to over five million, and food demand has risen accordingly. This review traces Oman's food-security trajectory over five decades, drawing on National Centre for Statistics and Information data, ministry production statistics reported in the national press, the Global Food Security Index and the scholarly literature. Over the period, coastal agriculture on the Al Batinah plain suffered severe groundwater salinisation, which undermined date and lime cultivation. Since about 2010, however, deliberate investment in fisheries, aquaculture, dairy, poultry, eggs and protected vegetables has raised overall food self-sufficiency to about 65.8% in 2024, with sector ratios of 144.5% for fish, 87.3% for animal production, 66.4% for food and beverage industries and 49.4% for agricultural crop products. At commodity level, the latest reported self-sufficiency is about 146–162% for fish, 97–99% for dates, 92–97% for milk and 92–95% for eggs, while poultry stood at 55–60% and red meat at 46% in 2023. Oman scored 71.2 in the 2022 GFSI (35th of 113), and its gain of 13.8 points since 2012 was the largest improvement of any country. The review concludes that Oman's combination of marine resources, targeted investment and port diversification offers a model for arid states, while water quality, climate risk and feed dependence remain the principal constraints.
