阿曼粮食安全五十年：渔业与灌溉农业的多元化自给之路

创新性研究

  1. Effects of waterborne exposure to crude mulberry leaf polysaccharides on lipopolysaccharide-induced acute inflammatory responses in gibel carp (*Carassius gibelio*): insights into host immune and intestinal responses（*Aquaculture*）

该研究的创新点在于采用水体暴露（waterborne exposure）途径递送桑叶粗多糖，而非传统的饲料添加方式，并同时考察宿主免疫与肠道响应两个层面。若结果证实水体暴露可有效调控LPS诱导的急性炎症，将为水产养殖中免疫增强剂的给药方式提供低成本、低应激的替代方案，对集约化养殖中的炎症性疾病管理具有潜在应用价值。

  1. Comparative analysis of immune responses reveals mechanisms underlying different susceptibility to *Cryptocaryon irritans* in large yellow croaker (*Larimichthys crocea*) and red sea bream (*Pagrus major*)（*Aquaculture*）

该研究以两种经济海水鱼为对象，通过比较免疫应答解析对刺激隐核虫易感性差异的机制，创新性体现在以“同病原、异宿主”的比较免疫学框架定位种间抗性差异的关键环节。研究结果有望为抗病育种标记筛选和物种混养策略提供理论依据，对海水网箱养殖中白点病的防控具有直接参考意义。

  1. Comparison of EBER flow FISH results from two facilities（*Cytometry Part B: Clinical Cytometry*）

该文虽属临床病毒学范畴，但其方法学核心——EBER flow FISH的跨机构可比性验证——对渔业免疫学中流式细胞术方法的标准化具有借鉴意义。多中心方法一致性评估的思路可迁移至鱼类免疫细胞表型分析的质量控制，提示本领域在方法学报告规范上仍有提升空间。

Food Security in the Sultanate of Oman, 1975–2025: Fisheries, Falaj Agriculture and the Rise of Diversified Self-Sufficiency
👤 Nosir, Walid 📅 2026-11-08
Oman differs from its Gulf neighbours in several respects relevant to food security: a long Arabian Sea coastline with productive fisheries, a monsoon-influenced southern region (Dhofar), an ancient irrigation tradition based on the falaj system, and major ports at Sohar, Duqm and Salalah that lie outside, or at the mouth of, the Strait of Hormuz. Since the start of the modern state in 1970, its population has grown from well under one million to over five million, and food demand has risen accordingly. This review traces Oman's food-security trajectory over five decades, drawing on National Centre for Statistics and Information data, ministry production statistics reported in the national press, the Global Food Security Index and the scholarly literature. Over the period, coastal agriculture on the Al Batinah plain suffered severe groundwater salinisation, which undermined date and lime cultivation. Since about 2010, however, deliberate investment in fisheries, aquaculture, dairy, poultry, eggs and protected vegetables has raised overall food self-sufficiency to about 65.8% in 2024, with sector ratios of 144.5% for fish, 87.3% for animal production, 66.4% for food and beverage industries and 49.4% for agricultural crop products. At commodity level, the latest reported self-sufficiency is about 146–162% for fish, 97–99% for dates, 92–97% for milk and 92–95% for eggs, while poultry stood at 55–60% and red meat at 46% in 2023. Oman scored 71.2 in the 2022 GFSI (35th of 113), and its gain of 13.8 points since 2012 was the largest improvement of any country. The review concludes that Oman's combination of marine resources, targeted investment and port diversification offers a model for arid states, while water quality, climate risk and feed dependence remain the principal constraints.
