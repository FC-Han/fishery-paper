本周研究概览

本周共收录渔业相关论文7篇，研究主题集中于鱼类入侵生态学、营养生态学、渔业海洋学及水产养殖基因组学等方向。其中，入侵生物学占据较大比重（3篇），涵盖全球尺度入侵风险评估、深海aphotic zone入侵记录及外来与本土鱼类的营养生态位重叠分析；此外，渔业资源变动与环境驱动因子的关联研究、鱼类黏膜免疫活性物质开发以及龙虾基因组资源综述亦各有涉及。整体来看，本周研究呈现出从传统资源评估向多学科交叉（机器学习、分子生物学、遥感技术）拓展的趋势，且对生态入侵与气候变化背景下渔业资源响应的关注度显著提升。

Abstract Nonbiodegradable, petroleum-based food packaging materials have been shown to cause significant environmental harm, particularly to aquatic ecosystems. As a sustainable alternative, nanocomposite films were developed from nanoemulsions stabilized by whey protein concentrate (WPC), incorporating a discontinuous phase rich in omega-3 oils and reinforced with titanium dioxide (TiO2) nanoparticles in two distinct morphologies: spheres and nanotubes. The effects of droplet size in the discontinuous phase and nanoreinforcement geometry on the films’ physical and mechanical properties were investigated. This difference in spatial distribution contributed to the enhanced structural integrity and overall performance of the nanotube-reinforced films. The film containing 0.2% TiO2 nanotubes had the best physical properties, highlighting its strong potential as a sustainable alternative to conventional petroleum-based plastics.

A total infectome framework for resolving complex disease etiology in aquaculture
👤 Xu, Yichun; Liu, Hanlin; Wu, Weichen; Gu, Yuchao; Zhang, Nai... 📅 2026-08-07
Abstract Traditional pathogen isolation can establish disease causality but often fails in complex infections involving unknown or multiple microbial backgrounds, whereas metatranscriptomics comprehensively captures the total infectome without directly resolving the primary etiological agent. Here, using overwintering syndrome (OWS) in grass carp, a widespread but unresolved aquaculture disease in China, as a disease model, we developed an integrated strategy that links comparative total infectome analysis with targeted isolation, experimental infection, and post-challenge infectome validation. Multi-organ metatranscriptomic profiling of healthy and diseased grass carp identified 107 microorganisms spanning viruses, bacteria, and eukaryotic microbes. Comparative infectomics prioritized Flavobacterium psychrophilum through its consistent disease enrichment, lesion-site abundance, multi-organ dissemination, and recurrence across affected hosts. Infection with F. psychrophilum alone reproduced the clinical signs, tissue pathology, mortality patterns, and infectome signatures of natural OWS, establishing it as the primary etiological agent. Temperature-shift experiments further showed that disease progression is promoted by warming from low temperature conditions, explaining the seasonal emergence of OWS. This work resolves a major grass carp disease and provides a generalizable framework for converting unbiased pathogen discovery into causal inference in complex infectious disease systems.

Marine Protein Sources and Lipid Levels in Microdiets Affect the Survival and Health of Whiteleg Shrimp (Penaeus vannamei) Post-Larvae in Clear-Water Recirculating Aquaculture Systems
👤 Barreto, André; Peixoto, Diogo; Fajardo, Carlos; Pires, Sílv... 📅 2026-08-05
This study evaluated the effects of different marine protein sources and dietary lipid levels in inert microdiets for whiteleg shrimp (Penaeus vannamei) post-larvae (PL) reared in clear-water recirculating aquaculture systems (RAS). Five diets were evaluated: a control diet containing a blend of fish and squid meals, diets based on fish meal, squid meal, or krill meal as the main marine ingredient, and a high-lipid fish meal-based formulation used to assess the effect of increased dietary lipid content. After 21 days, growth performance did not differ significantly among treatments. However, survival and physiological status were significantly affected by diet. Fatty acid composition reflected dietary inputs, with higher levels of n-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids in PL fed the high-lipid diet. Biomarker and gene expression analyses revealed modulation of antioxidant and immune responses in PL fed single-ingredient diets and the high-lipid formulation, suggesting increased physiological stress compared to the control and krill-based treatments. These results show that growth alone is not sufficient to assess diet adequacy. The use of combined marine protein sources or krill meal was associated with higher survival, while increasing dietary lipid content from 14 to 16% did not improve growth performance and was associated with reduced survival.

👤 Valdés, Marco; Quiñones, John; Cortes, Matias; Huaiquipán, R... 📅 2026-08-05
Aquaculture faces structural constraints linked to input sustainability, animal health, and a persistent reliance on fishmeal and fish oil, alongside the intensive use of antibiotics in high-density production systems. In this context, edible fungi have emerged as promising functional ingredients, primarily due to their content of structural polysaccharides, particularly β-glucans and chitin. This review synthesizes recent evidence on the dietary inclusion of fungal-derived compounds in aquafeeds for commercially relevant species. Overall, these bioactive components have been associated with beneficial effects on growth performance, feed efficiency, antioxidant status, and disease resistance in species such as Oncorhynchus mykiss, Salmo salar, and Oreochromis spp. However, the magnitude and consistency of these responses remain highly variable across studies. Consequently, their biological effects are context-dependent and should be interpreted according to ingredient characteristics, host species, dietary inclusion level, and experimental conditions rather than assuming a universal dose–response relationship. Effective implementation will require advances in matrix standardization, detailed structural characterization of bioactive compounds, and validation under industry-relevant conditions.

Adoption of Better Management Practices in Penaeus vannamei Aquaculture: A Critical Review with Special Reference to South India
👤 Kunda, Sumanth Kumar; Pulupu, SrinivasaRao 📅 2026-08-07
Better Management Practices (BMPs) have become a central instrument for disease prevention, resource efficiency, food-safety assurance and environmental improvement in Indian whiteleg shrimp aquaculture. This critical narrative review examines the technical content, adoption, determinants and reported outcomes of BMPs in Penaeus vannamei production, with particular attention to Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and the wider southern Indian coastline. Literature published from 2000 to 21 April 2026 was identified through accessible scholarly indexes, publisher records, citation searching and authoritative institutional sources, with earlier foundational studies retained where necessary. The evidence indicates that BMP adoption is substantial but uneven. Practices offering immediate and observable private returns, including feed control, seed screening and aeration management, generally face fewer adoption barriers than practices whose benefits depend on coordinated action, such as crop synchronisation, fallowing, shared water management and disease reporting. Indian disease-burden, efficiency and adoption studies demonstrate the scale of preventable loss and identify plausible management levers, but they do not isolate the causal effect of BMP adoption. Most adoption evidence remains cross-sectional, geographically concentrated and dependent on self-report. Objective residue testing and a randomised extension intervention from Vietnam provide stronger evidence that well-designed training can improve antimicrobial stewardship, although transferability to South India requires direct evaluation. The principal implementation constraints arise from unequal access to assets, trusted advice, collective organisation, traceability and risk finance. Future research should prioritise longitudinal, quasi-experimental and randomised evaluation; linkage of residue-testing, training and cluster records; and regionally representative studies outside Andhra Pradesh. Abstract Acoustic deterrents have been deployed with variable success to decrease fish interactions with anthropogenic hazards (i.e., power plant intakes, spillways) and prevent the spread of invasive species. Acoustic deterrents are nonphysical barriers that offer many advantages in that sound can travel a great distance, are relatively independent of light and weather conditions, do not interfere with navigation and are relatively inexpensive to implement. Assessing the efficacy of deterrents is challenging as there are few controlled or long-term studies and many reports fail to specify sound parameters (i.e., intensity, frequency) preventing independent assessment. It is imperative to factor fish sensory physiology when designing nonphysical deterrents as fish need to detect, localize and move away from the stimulus. The auditory sensitivity of the target species must be identified to optimize the acoustic stimulus and the ambient soundscape mapped to ensure the sound intensity will be effective. Animals may habituate to an aversive stimulus and therefore multimodal deterrents may prove more effective than single modal systems. This paper will provide an overview of acoustic deterrents, species targeted and their efficacy. Special emphasis will be given to efforts to block the upstream migration of invasive bigheaded carp in the Mississippi River basin that currently threatens the Laurentian Great Lakes. Recent studies using a multimodal acoustic deterrent that conditions fish to associate sound with carbon dioxide will be described.

👤 Marrable, I; Peoples, N; Kolmann, M A; Huie, J M; Figueira, ... 📅 2026-08-08
Abstract Specialised ecological niches can represent evolutionary traps that increase extinction risk when environments change. The consumption of coral tissue, mucus and skeleton is a rare feeding niche despite the abundance of coral on reefs, which raises questions about the evolutionary consequences of this dietary specialisation. In this study, we used phylogenetic comparative methods, micro-CT scanning, and scanning electron microscopy to explore the evolutionary history of corallivory across reef fishes and compare feeding morphology across species within a major clade of corallivorous species (Chaetodontidae) that vary in their coral consumption. We show that obligate corallivory has evolved independently at least 17 times but is an evolutionary “dead end” for most reef fish species. Early origination of facultative corallivory may have contributed to the success of butterflyfishes, the predominant clade of coral-feeding fishes, as they diversified in competitive reef environments. Obligate and facultative butterflyfish species differ primarily in dental morphology while overall cranial traits are largely similar between groups. Coral feeding requires continuous foraging with narrow energetic margins, and climate-driven disturbances occur markedly faster than evolutionary transitions away from corallivory. This temporal mismatch between change at ecological and evolutionary timescales subjects corallivorous fishes to heightened vulnerability as climate-driven disturbances increase in frequency and severity.
