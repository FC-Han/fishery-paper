本周研究概览

本周共收录渔业相关论文7篇，研究主题集中于鱼类入侵生态学、营养生态学、渔业海洋学及水产养殖基因组学等方向。其中，入侵生物学占据较大比重（3篇），涵盖全球尺度入侵风险评估、深海aphotic zone入侵记录及外来与本土鱼类的营养生态位重叠分析；此外，渔业资源变动与环境驱动因子的关联研究、鱼类黏膜免疫活性物质开发以及龙虾基因组资源综述亦各有涉及。整体来看，本周研究呈现出从传统资源评估向多学科交叉（机器学习、分子生物学、遥感技术）拓展的趋势，且对生态入侵与气候变化背景下渔业资源响应的关注度显著提升。

👤 Rodríguez Pineda, Lina M.; Ramos Ramos, María R.; Borroni,... 📅 2026-08-07
Abstract Nonbiodegradable, petroleum-based food packaging materials have been shown to cause significant environmental harm, particularly to aquatic ecosystems. As a sustainable alternative, nanocomposite films were developed from nanoemulsions stabilized by whey protein concentrate (WPC), incorporating a discontinuous phase rich in omega-3 oils and reinforced with titanium dioxide (TiO2) nanoparticles in two distinct morphologies: spheres and nanotubes. The effects of droplet size in the discontinuous phase and nanoreinforcement geometry on the films’ physical and mechanical properties were investigated. Film structure and nanoparticle distribution were characterized using scanning electron microscopy (SEM), energy-dispersive spectroscopy (EDS), and small- and wide-angle X-ray scattering (SAXS/WAXS). WPC demonstrated environmental adaptability in these nanostructured systems, producing films with uniform thickness, water vapor permeability (WVP), water content, and solubility across different formulations. Incorporation of TiO2, whether spherical or tubular, significantly enhanced the films’ light-blocking capabilities. Nanotube reinforcement also led to reduced opacity and proved more effective than spherical nanoparticles in strengthening the matrix. SEM and EDS analyses revealed a more homogeneous distribution of nanotubes throughout the polymer matrix, in contrast to the bottom-surface accumulation observed with spherical particles. This difference in spatial distribution contributed to the enhanced structural integrity and overall performance of the nanotube-reinforced films. The film containing 0.2% TiO2 nanotubes had the best physical properties, highlighting its strong potential as a sustainable alternative to conventional petroleum-based plastics.

A total infectome framework for resolving complex disease etiology in aquaculture
👤 Xu, Yichun; Liu, Hanlin; Wu, Weichen; Gu, Yuchao; Zhang, Nai... 📅 2026-08-07
Abstract Traditional pathogen isolation can establish disease causality but often fails in complex infections involving unknown or multiple microbial backgrounds, whereas metatranscriptomics comprehensively captures the total infectome without directly resolving the primary etiological agent. Here, using overwintering syndrome (OWS) in grass carp, a widespread but unresolved aquaculture disease in China, as a disease model, we developed an integrated strategy that links comparative total infectome analysis with targeted isolation, experimental infection, and post-challenge infectome validation. Multi-organ metatranscriptomic profiling of healthy and diseased grass carp identified 107 microorganisms spanning viruses, bacteria, and eukaryotic microbes. Comparative infectomics prioritized Flavobacterium psychrophilum through its consistent disease enrichment, lesion-site abundance, multi-organ dissemination, and recurrence across affected hosts. Infection with F. psychrophilum alone reproduced the clinical signs, tissue pathology, mortality patterns, and infectome signatures of natural OWS, establishing it as the primary etiological agent. Temperature-shift experiments further showed that disease progression is promoted by warming from low temperature conditions, explaining the seasonal emergence of OWS. This work resolves a major grass carp disease and provides a generalizable framework for converting unbiased pathogen discovery into causal inference in complex infectious disease systems.

Adoption of Better Management Practices in Penaeus vannamei Aquaculture: A Critical Review with Special Reference to South India
👤 Kunda, Sumanth Kumar; Pulupu, SrinivasaRao 📅 2026-08-07
Better Management Practices (BMPs) have become a central instrument for disease prevention, resource efficiency, food-safety assurance and environmental improvement in Indian whiteleg shrimp aquaculture. This critical narrative review examines the technical content, adoption, determinants and reported outcomes of BMPs in Penaeus vannamei production, with particular attention to Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu and the wider southern Indian coastline. Literature published from 2000 to 21 April 2026 was identified through accessible scholarly indexes, publisher records, citation searching and authoritative institutional sources, with earlier foundational studies retained where necessary. The evidence indicates that BMP adoption is substantial but uneven. Practices offering immediate and observable private returns, including feed control, seed screening and aeration management, generally face fewer adoption barriers than practices whose benefits depend on coordinated action, such as crop synchronisation, fallowing, shared water management and disease reporting. Indian disease-burden, efficiency and adoption studies demonstrate the scale of preventable loss and identify plausible management levers, but they do not isolate the causal effect of BMP adoption. Most adoption evidence remains cross-sectional, geographically concentrated and dependent on self-report. Objective residue testing and a randomised extension intervention from Vietnam provide stronger evidence that well-designed training can improve antimicrobial stewardship, although transferability to South India requires direct evaluation. The principal implementation constraints arise from unequal access to assets, trusted advice, collective organisation, traceability and risk finance. Future research should prioritise longitudinal, quasi-experimental and randomised evaluation; linkage of residue-testing, training and cluster records; and regionally representative studies outside Andhra Pradesh. BMP policy is most likely to be effective when information and finance support practices with private returns, while collective-benefit practices are reinforced through credible group governance and carefully designed market or insurance incentives.

To assess how these pressures influence biodiversity detection, we employed eDNA metabarcoding targeting the 12S rRNA gene to survey fish diversity across 10 sites, integrating both surface water and sediment matrices. A total of 40 species belonging to 16 orders, 27 families and 32 genera, were identified. Among them, L. crocea, Bostrychus sinensis, Clupanodon thrissa and Siganus fuscescens are the dominate species, which comprised over half of all OTUs. While eDNA effectively captured the dominance of cultured species like L. crocea, the extreme biomass signal poses challenges for detecting wild populations. Our findings underscore that a multi-matrix eDNA approach is crucial for accurate biodiversity assessments in aquaculture-dominated coastal ecosystems, although distinguishing wild stocks from aquaculture-derived DNA remains a critical methodological hurdle.

Similarly, severe renal damage occurred at 800 mg/L, featuring marked vascular congestion, melanomacrophage aggregation, and widespread tubular necrosis. Overall, these findings demonstrate that acute waterborne COP exposure induces severe hepatotoxicity and nephrotoxicity in Betta splendens driven by oxidative stress, pro-inflammatory signaling, and apoptotic pathways. This study provides essential baseline toxicity thresholds and highlights the physiological risks COP spills pose to tropical freshwater labyrinth fish.
