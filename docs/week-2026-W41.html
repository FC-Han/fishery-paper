智能渔业与鱼类研究的多维创新进展

本周研究概览

本周共收录5篇渔业相关论文，研究方向较为分散，涵盖渔业技术现代化、鱼类行为分析、渔业考古、替代蛋白源开发以及鱼类微生物组学。整体来看，本周论文呈现出明显的跨学科特征：一方面，自动化、电子监控（electronic monitoring, EM）与人工智能（AI）在捕捞业中的应用受到持续关注；另一方面，鱼类健康与饲料可持续性问题仍是水产养殖领域的核心议题。值得注意的是，本周缺乏发表在顶级综合性期刊上的高影响力成果，研究更多集中于专业领域期刊，反映出渔业科学本周以应用导向和方法创新为主。

创新性研究

  1. From Mechanized Longlines to Smart Fisheries: Integrating Automation, Electronic Monitoring, and Artificial Intelligence（Journal of Marine Science and Engineering）

该综述系统整合了71篇文献，梳理了延绳钓渔业从机械化到智能化的技术演进路径，重点讨论了自动化、EM与AI的协同整合。其创新之处在于将分散的技术进展纳入统一框架，为智能渔业（smart fisheries）的落地提供了系统性参考，对渔业管理与合规监测具有潜在政策意义。

  1. Fish 3D Clustering App (F3DCA): A Non-Supervised Clustering Method with a Machine Learning Autoencoder for Analyzing 3D Trajectories of Three Fish Species（Biology）

该研究开发了一种基于无监督学习与自编码器（autoencoder）的3D轨迹聚类工具，用于分析三种鱼类的运动行为。其创新点在于将深度学习降维与聚类相结合，减少了对人工标注的依赖，为鱼类行为表型分析及神经退行性过程研究提供了可推广的方法学工具。

  1. Bacterial Taxonomic Profiling of Freshwater Fish Across Hydrographic Basins of Kazakhstan（Fishes）

该研究对哈萨克斯坦六大水文盆地、六个科的193尾野生淡水鱼进行了多组织（鳃、肾、脾、肝）细菌群落测序分析，填补了中亚地区鱼类微生物组数据的空白。其创新性在于大空间尺度的跨流域采样设计，为理解环境与宿主因素对鱼类微生物群落的影响提供了基础数据。

  1. Production of African House Fly (Musca Domestica) Maggots using Decomposed Wastes, Cow Dung and Sawdust as a Sustainable Protein Source for Poultry, Fish and Pig Feeds（International Journal For Multidisciplinary Research）

该研究探索了利用分解废弃物、牛粪和锯末培养家蝇（Musca domestica）幼虫作为替代蛋白源的可行性，以应对乌干达鱼粉和大豆粕成本上升的问题。其创新点在于将废弃物管理与饲料生产相结合，具有循环经济与可持续水产养殖的双重意义，但研究深度与规模化验证仍需加强。

An Early Neolithic Burial with a Miniature Fish-Shaped Bait on the Shore of Lake Baikal
👤 Kichigin, D. E.; Kharinsky, A. V. 📅 2026-10-05
We introduce finds from burial 3 at Khuzhir-Nuge-9 in the Olkhon area. Data on the location, above-ground burial structure (oval pavement ~3.5 m in diameter), grave (oval, 37 cm deep pit), funerary rite (inhumation on the right side with flexed legs, head oriented toward the north, burial strewn with ocher), and grave goods are provided. Clusters of tools and separate stone and bone artifacts, found in different parts of the grave, are described. Typical items include a robust bone tool, an end-scraper, microblades, and retouched and unretouched flakes made from jasper, chalcedony, flint, etc. The most expressive find is a miniature bait made from chert and shaped like a fish. The distribution area of stone figurines of fish in the Baikal area is outlined, their dates are provided, and opinions about their function are cited. Experts view these peculiar items as fishing baits. On the basis of the radiocarbon date of the burial and other sites on Lake Baikal shore, we conclude that such baits were used in this area from the Early Neolithic to the Middle Neolithic, i.e., in the 6th and 5th millennia BC.

Fish 3D Clustering App (F3DCA): A Non-Supervised Clustering Method with a Machine Learning Autoencoder for Analyzing 3D Trajectories of Three Fish Species
👤 Farhan, Ali; Audira, Gilbert; Luong, Cao Thang; Hung, Chih-H... 📅 2026-10-05
Fish behaviors are widely studied in various research fields due to their relevance to many aspects, including neurodegenerative processes, since they can impair motor control, complex behavior, habituation, and response to environmental stimuli. 3D locomotion trajectory analysis is one of the assays to analyze complex behaviors in fish. However, while existing 3D tracking software can capture the 3D trajectories of fish, they still rely heavily on manual or semi-automated analysis. Moreover, traditional analysis methods are inadequate for handling the high complexity of raw 3D data (X, Y, Z coordinates), and manual/semi-automated procedures introduce subjective human bias and are highly time-consuming, highlighting the need for a fully automated, unsupervised machine learning method. Here, we aimed to develop an unsupervised clustering method with machine learning to automatically analyze fish behaviors by using fish 3D coordinates. To evaluate the performance of this method, five different datasets, including (1) interspecies fish that consisted of three fish species, which were Danio rerio, Oryzias woworae, and Kryptopterus vitreolus, (2) shoal sex composition, (3) caudal fin amputation effect, (4) habituation time effect, and (5) acute ethanol exposure effect on zebrafish behaviors, were analyzed. The current method utilizes multiple clustering algorithms, including K-Means, BIRCH, Gaussian Mixture Model, and Spectral Clustering, which were then applied to the 3D trajectory data in every dataset. Afterward, the clustering performance was evaluated using the Silhouette Score and Davies–Bouldin Index, followed by the use of deep-learning autoencoders to improve latent feature representation and enhance cluster separation. The clustering outcomes were further compared with predefined experimental groups using ARI and NMI and were qualitatively interpreted alongside conventional behavioral endpoints. External agreement varied among datasets and clustering algorithms. The proposed method can characterize differences in zebrafish 3D locomotor trajectory patterns across experimental conditions, while its separation power may decrease when behavioral differences are subtle or highly overlapping. Furthermore, the present study evaluated the method using multiple behavioral datasets; a similar framework can be extended to zebrafish models of neurodegenerative disease to identify disease-associated alterations in movement patterns and neurobehavioral organization. Finally, a user-friendly software package with easy installation and readable trajectory-analysis outputs was also developed that can be useful in biomedical research.

Bacterial Taxonomic Profiling of Freshwater Fish Across Hydrographic Basins of Kazakhstan
👤 Kumar, Marat; Kasymbekov, Yermukhammet; Khan, Yelizaveta; Su... 📅 2026-10-05
Bacterial taxonomic profiles associated with freshwater fish in Kazakhstan remain poorly characterized. We characterized bacterial sequence profiles from 193 apparently healthy wild freshwater fish representing six families and six major hydrographic basins. Gill, kidney, spleen, and liver tissues were combined into 20 pooled samples, and RNA-derived double-stranded cDNA libraries were analyzed for bacterial taxonomic assignments. Pseudomonadota (51.9%) and Actinomycetota (29.5%) showed the highest mean relative sequence abundances, followed by Bacillota (8.3%), Bacteroidota (4.9%), and Fusobacteriota (2.7%). Alpha diversity did not differ significantly among basins. In separate PERMANOVA models, basin accounted for 33.6% of variation in genus-level profiles but was not significant (R2 = 0.336, p = 0.0555), while fish family accounted for 25.3% (R2 = 0.253, p = 0.5472). In the joint model, basin showed a significant marginal effect after accounting for fish family (R2 = 0.346, p = 0.0338), whereas fish family remained nonsignificant. Seventeen genera met the operational core criteria. Selected potentially health-relevant sequence assignments included Escherichia coli, Salmonella enterica, Aeromonas spp., and Stenotrophomonas maltophilia. These sequence-based detections do not establish organism viability or active infection. Overall, these findings provide baseline information on fish-associated bacterial taxonomic profiles across Kazakhstan’s major freshwater systems.
📰 International Journal For Multidisciplinary Research
💡 为缓解乌干达鱼粉和豆粕价格高企对畜禽及水产养殖的制约，本研究评估了利用非洲家蝇（Musca domestica）进行低成本蝇蛆生产的可行性。将市场废弃物厌氧分解7–18天后暴露于野生家蝇自然产卵，卵基物转入开放容器并与牛粪、锯末混合促进幼虫生长。7–14天内幼虫长至0.5–1.0英寸，平均10 kg废弃基质可产2–4 kg鲜蛆，直接饲喂禽猪适口性良好，多余蝇蛆冷冻保存后解冻饲喂。该系统简单、低成本、环境友好，可将市场废弃物转化为优质蛋白，适合乌干达养殖户推广。
Production of African House Fly (Musca Domestica) Maggots using Decomposed Wastes, Cow Dung and Sawdust as a Sustainable Protein Source for Poultry, Fish and Pig Feeds:
👤 Akol, Benard 📅 2026-10-05
The escalating cost of fishmeal and soybean meal threatens poultry, fish and pig production in Uganda. While poor waste management is affecting fish production in lakes and rivers, human and animal feeds require the same fish. Farmers involved in maggot production, are using black soldier flies. This study evaluated a low-cost maggot production using African house flies that are resistant to the temperature variations. Market waste were collected from Awindiri Market dustbin, crushed and placed in a sealed drum for anaerobic decomposition for 7-18 days. The decomposed matter was exposed to wild African house flies (Musca domestica) for natural oviposition. Egg-laden substrate was transferred to open rearing containers and mixed with cow dung and sawdust to enhance growth. Maggots grew to 0.5 to 1.0 inch within 7-14 days, harvested by sieving, sorting and washing. Average 10 kg of waste substrate yielded between 2-4 kg of fresh maggots. Maggots were fed directly to poultry and pigs, showing high palatability. Excess maggots were preserved in a freezer and thawed at 21-36°C before feeding, ensuring continuous availability. The system is simple, low-cost, environmentally friendly, converting market waste into high-quality protein. It is recommended for adoption by farmers in Uganda.

From Mechanized Longlines to Smart Fisheries: Integrating Automation, Electronic Monitoring, and Artificial Intelligence
👤 Kwon, Inyeong; Hwang, Bo-Kyu; Lee, Jihoon 📅 2026-10-05
Longline fisheries harvest high-value pelagic and demersal species but remain labor-intensive and require reliable monitoring of catches, bycatch, and fishing effort. This technology-oriented narrative review synthesizes 71 sources on mechanization, automation, electronic monitoring (EM), artificial intelligence (AI), and prospective smart fisheries. Evidence is distinguished by source type, application setting, and operational maturity. Mechanized hauling, baiting, and integrated autoline systems support fishing operations, whereas EM provides records for catch verification and management. Computer vision studies demonstrate catch-event detection, species classification, and selected compliance-monitoring tasks, but reported performance remains specific to the datasets and validation conditions. Commercial availability, experimental performance, and conceptual feasibility are therefore assessed separately. Edge AI, integrated intelligent reporting, digital twins, and generative AI remain emerging or prospective for longline fisheries in the reviewed evidence. The synthesis identifies camera visibility, cross-vessel generalization, rare-species detection, interoperability, and data governance as key constraints. A practical development pathway combines established equipment and EM with externally validated AI, expert review, and feedback to subsequent vessel operations. Increased fishing efficiency alone does not establish sustainability; environmental and management outcomes require separate evaluation.
