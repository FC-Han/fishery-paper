水产养殖可持续发展与鱼类健康研究前沿

本周研究概览

本周共收录渔业相关论文17篇，研究主题涵盖气候变化对渔业资源的影响、渔业数据整合与种群评估、水产养殖环境效应与病害监测、微塑料污染、鱼类检测技术及渔业社会经济等多个方向。整体来看，本周研究呈现出两个显著趋势：一是气候变化与人类活动对渔业生态系统的复合影响受到持续关注，尤其是海洋热浪引发的生态连锁反应；二是方法学创新突出，包括将渔民知识纳入种群评估、物理信息神经网络用于海洋浮标轨迹预测、以及无酸微波消解等绿色分析化学方法。此外，微塑料污染在水产养殖和海产品中的分布与风险仍是热点议题。

高影响力期刊亮点

PNAS 发表了两篇值得关注的论文。其一，*A Bering Sea marine heatwave triggers unprecedented crab hybridization, challenging fisheries management*，记录了白令海海洋热浪发生7年后出现的意外生态响应——先前未见的蟹类杂交现象，揭示了海洋热浪通过延迟性生态途径产生管理挑战的机制，对渔业资源管理具有重要警示意义。其二，*Biodiversity supports coastal nutrient cycling with a stable source of fish excretion*，探讨了生物多样性对鱼类排泄物养分供给稳定性的支撑作用，为理解生物多样性与生态系统功能的关系提供了新证据。

fishFingers: Prioritization of High-Resolution Mass Spectrometry Features by Prediction of Bioconcentration Factors in Fish Species
👤 Szabo, Drew; MacOscar, Kian; Plaisted, Katie; McGarrigle, Ma... 📅 2026-10-24
Abstract The number of novel entities produced around the world exceeds our capacity to evaluate the risk of each chemical under traditional frameworks. Detecting, prioritizing, and identifying thousands of chemicals present in the environment is a challenge to understanding this planetary boundary. Machine learning has demonstrated quick and accurate predictions for ecotoxicological end points for chemicals with structural fingerprints derived from high-resolution mass spectrometry (HRMS). This study provides a new nontargeted screening strategy to prioritize HRMS features by the prediction of bioconcentration factors (BCF) without a priori structural annotation. An XGBoost model was trained on a database with structural fingerprints from 1285 unique substances and log10 BCF measurements from 111 species of fish. Validation was performed using structural fingerprints derived from high-quality tandem mass spectrometry (MS2), with RMSE = 1.02, MAE = 0.81, and ρ = 0.50, demonstrating its capability in predicting log10 BCF and to rank HRMS features by predicted bioconcentration from a wide chemical space. Variations in BCF were observed in species such as common carp (Cyprinus carpio), which exhibits lower average BCF compared with zebrafish (Danio rerio). Halogenation, aliphatic and aromatic carbon, and heteroatomaticity were among the most important structural fingerprints for BCF. The model and package are available from https://github.com/drewszabo/fishFingers.

A Greener Acid-free Microwave-Assisted Digestion Method for Multielement Analysis of Fish Tissues by ICP–MS
👤 Pereira, Marcos V. S.; Fonseca, Elisânia K. B.; de Jesus, J... 📅 2026-10-19
Abstract Trace element monitoring in fish tissues requires reliable and sustainable sample preparation methods, particularly for complex biological matrices. In this study, an acid-free microwave-assisted digestion (AFMAD) method based exclusively on H2O2 was developed and optimized for multielement determination in fish tissues by ICP–MS. A central composite design coupled with response surface methodology was employed to evaluate the influence of H2O2 concentration, ramp time, and digestion temperature on matrix decomposition efficiency using 100 mg of dry fish tissue subsamples throughout the method development. The optimized conditions consisted of 30% (v/v)H2O2, 8 min ramp time, a 20 min hold time and 240 °C, resulting in residual carbon contents below 20%, which indicates efficient oxidation of the organic matrix using H2O2 in the absence of HNO3. The analytical performance of the proposed method was evaluated using cod muscle certified reference material (BCR-422), providing recoveries ranging from 76 to 101% for Fe, Cu, Zn, Mn, As, and Cd. Excellent linearity was obtained for all analytes (R2 &gt; 0.990) within the 0–50 μg L–1 range. Limits of detection ranged from 0.0004 to 0.04 μg g–1, demonstrating suitable sensitivity for trace elemental analysis in fish matrices. The applicability of the method was demonstrated through the analysis of five commercial fish species collected in the Mariana-MG region. Concentrations of toxic elements were found in Megaleporinus conirostris (Cd, 0.003 mg kg–1) and Cichla ocellaris (As, 0.07 mg kg–1) and remained below the maximum limits established by Brazilian legislation. The proposed AFMAD method demonstrated satisfactory analytical performance for complex fish matrices while eliminating the use of concentrated mineral acids, reducing hazardous reagent consumption, and improving the sustainability characteristics of sample preparation procedures for elemental analysis.

Ice is commonly used, but storage space, procurement, transportation, and handling may pose challenges for fishers operating small vessels. This study examined fishers’ assessments of a 60 L refrigerated cool box as a catch-handling technology in Cilincing, North Jakarta. Sex-Specific DNA Methylation in the Liver of Ancherythroculter nigrocauda, a Fish with Female-Biased Size Dimorphism
👤 Chen, Jian; Li, Pei; Luo, Lifei; Guo, Hongxi; Sun, Yanhong 📅 2026-10-20
Sexual size dimorphism (SSD) is widespread in teleost fishes, yet the epigenetic mechanisms underlying sex-specific growth remain poorly understood, particularly in non-model species. Here, we performed genome-wide DNA methylation profiling of liver tissue in Ancherythroculter nigrocauda, a species exhibiting female-biased SSD, using the MethylRAD approach. MethylRAD sequencing of five females and five males generated approximately 1.0–1.5 million unique methylated loci per sample. Comparative analysis identified 82,872 differentially methylated sites (DMSs), which clearly separated sexes and were significantly enriched in promoters and CpG islands. Gene ontology analyses revealed that sex-biased methylation preferentially affected pathways related to intracellular signaling, cell adhesion, cytoskeletal organization, and protein modification, processes closely associated with hepatic growth and metabolic regulation. Chromosomal binning uncovered large contiguous male-biased methylation domains on Superscaffold15 and Superscaffold20, consistent with recombination-suppressed regions and suggesting candidate sex-linked loci, with Superscaffold20 supported by prior sex-specific coverage differences. Integration with liver transcriptome data revealed a threshold-dependent regulatory architecture: genes harboring dense methylation clustering (>20 DMS) were approximately six-fold more likely to exhibit sex-biased expression, and directional concordance between methylation and transcription further supported functional coupling. Together, these findings demonstrate that sex-biased DNA methylation in the liver is widespread yet concentrated within regulatory hotspots and discrete chromosomal domains, highlighting epigenetic regulation as a key contributor to hepatic sexual dimorphism and providing new insights into the molecular basis of SSD in teleost fishes.

Effects of Fish Farming Prior to Farm Removal on Macrozoobenthic Crustacean and Molluscan Assemblages in a Major Turkish Aquaculture Gulf: Integrating Historical Data with Literature Insights
👤 Aslan, Berivan Elif; Mutlu, Erhan 📅 2026-10-19
Fish farming can significantly impact benthic ecosystems, particularly through alterations in water and sediment quality, potentially disrupting ecosystem functioning and biodiversity. This study investigates the effects of fish-farming on crustacean and molluscan assemblages in Güllük Gulf and Bozburun bay along Turkey’s Aegean coast. Macrozoobenthic samples were collected from fish farm sites and corresponding control sites, representing a baseline prior to the relocation of many farms to offshore areas in the 2010s. A total of 114 crustacean and 54 molluscan species were identified at farm sites, compared to 88 crustaceans and 52 molluscs at control sites. Crustaceans, particularly amphipods, were the most diverse group, while bivalves dominated the molluscan assemblages. Species such as Moerella donacina and Varicorbula gibba (Bivalvia) were most abundant at farm sites, whereas Chondrochelia savignyi (Tanaidacea) and Apseudopsis latreillii (Tanaidacea) were dominant at control sites. Ecological differences between Güllük Gulf and Bozburun bay, as well as between farm and control sites, were evident, with faunal abundance at farm sites showing stronger correlations with environmental variables, including orthophosphate, silicate, and temperature. Biomass emerged as a key indicator for assessing ecological status, alongside abundance and species composition. Fish-farm capacity, as determined by the number of fish cages, played a significant role in shaping faunal patterns, with higher capacity farms exhibiting greater ecological disturbance. This study provides a crucial baseline for future research on the long-term ecological impacts of fish farming, including ecosystem recovery and the role of environmental variables. The findings suggest the importance of incorporating biomass-based indicators and considering farm capacity and site-specific conditions in fish-farming management strategies to mitigate environmental degradation.

👤 Zhang, Lingkai; Chen, Wei; Gao, Xinyu; Cai, Huaiyuan; Zhou, ... 📅 2026-10-22
ABSTRACT Influenza A virus produces three RNA species—viral RNA (vRNA), complementary RNA (cRNA), and messenger RNA (mRNA)—whose synthesis and trafficking are spatially compartmentalized. However, the high sequence similarity between cRNA and mRNA hinders their distinction in situ , limiting understanding of the spatial organization of the viral replication-transcription dynamics. Here, triple-lock fluorescence in situ hybridization (TriLock-FISH) was developed to distinguish vRNA, cRNA, and mRNA in situ with high specificity. TriLock-FISH enabled a genome-wide, time-resolved single-cell atlas of RNA abundance and localization, revealing marked cell-to-cell variation in RNA abundance but relatively conserved spatial organization. cRNA was found to be predominantly nuclear, with a small but reproducible cytoplasmic fraction. Application to infected mouse lung tissue demonstrated simultaneous spatial detection of all viral RNA species. This approach provides a highly specific framework for single-cell analysis of RNA virus replication and transcription. IMPORTANCE Influenza virus relies on three closely related RNA molecules for replication and gene expression, yet distinguishing them inside infected cells has remained technically challenging. This limitation has hindered understanding of viral replication in space and time. We developed triple-lock fluorescence in situ hybridization, which enables simultaneous visualization of all three viral RNA species across all eight influenza genome segments in infected cells and tissues. Single-cell analysis revealed striking heterogeneity in viral RNA abundance across infected cells, whereas intracellular RNA localization patterns remained relatively conserved. Complementary RNA, a replication intermediate long thought to be strictly nuclear, was also detected at low levels in the cytoplasm. These findings provide new insight into influenza virus replication and establish a broadly applicable tool for studying RNA virus biology.
