渔业资源可持续管理与生态风险研究

本周研究概览

本周共收录渔业相关论文17篇，研究主题涵盖气候变化对渔业资源的影响、渔业数据整合与种群评估、水产养殖环境效应与病害监测、微塑料污染、鱼类检测技术及渔业社会经济等多个方向。整体来看，本周研究呈现出两个显著趋势：一是气候变化与人类活动对渔业生态系统的复合影响受到持续关注，尤其是海洋热浪引发的生态连锁反应；二是方法学创新突出，包括将渔民知识纳入种群评估、物理信息神经网络用于海洋浮标轨迹预测、以及无酸微波消解等绿色分析化学方法。此外，微塑料污染在水产养殖和海产品中的分布与风险仍是热点议题。

高影响力期刊亮点

PNAS 发表了两篇值得关注的论文。其一，*A Bering Sea marine heatwave triggers unprecedented crab hybridization, challenging fisheries management*，记录了白令海海洋热浪发生7年后出现的意外生态响应——先前未见的蟹类杂交现象，揭示了海洋热浪通过延迟性生态途径产生管理挑战的机制，对渔业资源管理具有重要警示意义。其二，*Biodiversity supports coastal nutrient cycling with a stable source of fish excretion*，探讨了生物多样性对鱼类排泄物养分供给稳定性的支撑作用，为理解生物多样性与生态系统功能的关系提供了新证据。

Communications Sustainability 刊发了 *Hidden population declines revealed by integrating long-term fisheries data with fishers' knowledge*，该研究将1972–2022年长期渔业统计数据与渔民知识相结合，揭示了"技术蠕变"（technological creep）如何掩盖种群下降并偏倚渔业评估，为改进渔业资源评估方法提供了重要思路。

A Greener Acid-free Microwave-Assisted Digestion Method for Multielement Analysis of Fish Tissues by ICP–MS
👤 Pereira, Marcos V. S.; Fonseca, Elisânia K. B.; de Jesus, J... 📅 2026-10-19
Abstract Trace element monitoring in fish tissues requires reliable and sustainable sample preparation methods, particularly for complex biological matrices. In this study, an acid-free microwave-assisted digestion (AFMAD) method based exclusively on H2O2 was developed and optimized for multielement determination in fish tissues by ICP–MS. A central composite design coupled with response surface methodology was employed to evaluate the influence of H2O2 concentration, ramp time, and digestion temperature on matrix decomposition efficiency using 100 mg of dry fish tissue subsamples throughout the method development. The optimized conditions consisted of 30% (v/v)H2O2, 8 min ramp time, a 20 min hold time and 240 °C, resulting in residual carbon contents below 20%, which indicates efficient oxidation of the organic matrix using H2O2 in the absence of HNO3. The analytical performance of the proposed method was evaluated using cod muscle certified reference material (BCR-422), providing recoveries ranging from 76 to 101% for Fe, Cu, Zn, Mn, As, and Cd. Excellent linearity was obtained for all analytes (R2 &gt; 0.990) within the 0–50 μg L–1 range. Limits of detection ranged from 0.0004 to 0.04 μg g–1, demonstrating suitable sensitivity for trace elemental analysis in fish matrices. The applicability of the method was demonstrated through the analysis of five commercial fish species collected in the Mariana-MG region. Concentrations of toxic elements were found in Megaleporinus conirostris (Cd, 0.003 mg kg–1) and Cichla ocellaris (As, 0.07 mg kg–1) and remained below the maximum limits established by Brazilian legislation. The proposed AFMAD method demonstrated satisfactory analytical performance for complex fish matrices while eliminating the use of concentrated mineral acids, reducing hazardous reagent consumption, and improving the sustainability characteristics of sample preparation procedures for elemental analysis.

Sex-Specific DNA Methylation in the Liver of Ancherythroculter nigrocauda, a Fish with Female-Biased Size Dimorphism
👤 Chen, Jian; Li, Pei; Luo, Lifei; Guo, Hongxi; Sun, Yanhong 📅 2026-10-20
Sexual size dimorphism (SSD) is widespread in teleost fishes, yet the epigenetic mechanisms underlying sex-specific growth remain poorly understood, particularly in non-model species. Here, we performed genome-wide DNA methylation profiling of liver tissue in Ancherythroculter nigrocauda, a species exhibiting female-biased SSD, using the MethylRAD approach. MethylRAD sequencing of five females and five males generated approximately 1.0–1.5 million unique methylated loci per sample. Comparative analysis identified 82,872 differentially methylated sites (DMSs), which clearly separated sexes and were significantly enriched in promoters and CpG islands. Gene ontology analyses revealed that sex-biased methylation preferentially affected pathways related to intracellular signaling, cell adhesion, cytoskeletal organization, and protein modification, processes closely associated with hepatic growth and metabolic regulation. Chromosomal binning uncovered large contiguous male-biased methylation domains on Superscaffold15 and Superscaffold20, consistent with recombination-suppressed regions and suggesting candidate sex-linked loci, with Superscaffold20 supported by prior sex-specific coverage differences. Integration with liver transcriptome data revealed a threshold-dependent regulatory architecture: genes harboring dense methylation clustering (>20 DMS) were approximately six-fold more likely to exhibit sex-biased expression, and directional concordance between methylation and transcription further supported functional coupling. Together, these findings demonstrate that sex-biased DNA methylation in the liver is widespread yet concentrated within regulatory hotspots and discrete chromosomal domains, highlighting epigenetic regulation as a key contributor to hepatic sexual dimorphism and providing new insights into the molecular basis of SSD in teleost fishes.

Effects of Fish Farming Prior to Farm Removal on Macrozoobenthic Crustacean and Molluscan Assemblages in a Major Turkish Aquaculture Gulf: Integrating Historical Data with Literature Insights
👤 Aslan, Berivan Elif; Mutlu, Erhan 📅 2026-10-19
Fish farming can significantly impact benthic ecosystems, particularly through alterations in water and sediment quality, potentially disrupting ecosystem functioning and biodiversity. This study investigates the effects of fish-farming on crustacean and molluscan assemblages in Güllük Gulf and Bozburun bay along Turkey’s Aegean coast. Macrozoobenthic samples were collected from fish farm sites and corresponding control sites, representing a baseline prior to the relocation of many farms to offshore areas in the 2010s. A total of 114 crustacean and 54 molluscan species were identified at farm sites, compared to 88 crustaceans and 52 molluscs at control sites. Crustaceans, particularly amphipods, were the most diverse group, while bivalves dominated the molluscan assemblages. Species such as Moerella donacina and Varicorbula gibba (Bivalvia) were most abundant at farm sites, whereas Chondrochelia savignyi (Tanaidacea) and Apseudopsis latreillii (Tanaidacea) were dominant at control sites. Biomass emerged as a key indicator for assessing ecological status, alongside abundance and species composition. Fish-farm capacity, as determined by the number of fish cages, played a significant role in shaping faunal patterns, with higher capacity farms exhibiting greater ecological disturbance. This study provides a crucial baseline for future research on the long-term ecological impacts of fish farming, including ecosystem recovery and the role of environmental variables. The findings suggest the importance of incorporating biomass-based indicators and considering farm capacity and site-specific conditions in fish-farming management strategies to mitigate environmental degradation.

Marker-Centered Evaluation of De Novo Assemblers Using Validated Microsatellite Loci in the Endangered Korean Fish Pseudopungtungia nigra
👤 Kim, Kang-Rae; Kim, Keun-Sik; Bang, In-Chul 📅 2026-10-20
The recovery of experimentally validated microsatellite loci from short-read assemblies may provide an application-specific measure of assembly quality that complements conventional genome-wide statistics. In this study, four de novo assemblers—SPAdes, ABySS, SOAPdenovo2, and MaSuRCA—were evaluated using genomic reads from two individuals of the endangered Korean freshwater fish Pseudopungtungia nigra. Assembler performance was assessed based on assembly contiguity, BUSCO completeness, computational requirements, and the recovery of 20 PCR-validated microsatellite loci and their flanking regions. SPAdes produced the most contiguous assemblies and the highest BUSCO completeness among the tested assemblers, with N50 values of 5.5–7.0 kb and BUSCO completeness of 30.2–35.2%, and recovered all 20 loci and their flanking sequences at high confidence in both individuals. Marker recovery by ABySS, SOAPdenovo2, and MaSuRCA varied according to k-mer size and sample, with high-confidence recovery decreasing at larger k-mer sizes in several configurations and reaching 9 of 20 loci in the least favorable condition. SPAdes used 32.1–34.2 GB of peak memory, compared with up to 135.0 GB in the most memory-intensive SOAPdenovo2 runs. Reference-guided comparisons retained substantially greater total aligned sequence with the two Pseudorasbora references than with the more divergent Cyprinus carpio reference, although the mean identity of the smaller retained C. carpio alignment set was slightly higher. All 20 validated marker sequences produced high-confidence provisional placements in both Pseudorasbora reference genomes. As an exploratory downstream application, the SUC6832-derived pseudo-reference enabled joint genotyping of the two samples and yielded 2,012,643 filtered candidate variants across 25 guide-assigned scaffolds. These results indicate that SPAdes provided the most favorable balance of validated marker recovery, relative assembly quality, and memory efficiency among the tested configurations, although all assemblies remained fragmented at the genome-wide level. Marker-centered benchmarking can therefore complement conventional assembly statistics when short-read assemblies are generated primarily for microsatellite development, while pseudo-reference-based genomic analyses should remain provisional because of assembly fragmentation, reference bias, and limited biological sampling.

Management implications of misspecification in observed recreational removals for reef fish and coastal pelagic stocks
👤 Peterson, Cassidy D; Sagarese, Skyler R; Vaughan, Nathan R.;... 📅 2026-10-19
Recreational removals of fishes are particularly challenging to estimate accurately. A recent pilot study indicated that U.S. East Coast recreational removals may be overestimated by 30-40%, which is concerning in the Southeast, where recreational removals often exceed commercial. Management strategy evaluation was used to identify the management implications of recreational removal misspecification on nine reef and coastal pelagic fishes in the U.S. Gulf of America. The R package SSMSE was used to test performance of “Status Quo” management across seven removal misspecification scenarios. When recreational removals were misspecified, absolute stock magnitude was not estimated accurately. However, relative status reference points were generally well estimated when there was constant or randomly varying misspecification (representative relative index of removals), such that Status Quo management was still able to meet biomass, exploitation, and yield targets. The ability to identify reference points and manage the stock was adversely impacted by abrupt shifts or annually time-varying trends in misspecification (non-representative index of removals). Results emphasize that a relative index of recreational removals is generally sufficient to successfully manage a stock.

Behrangi’s The Little Black Fish: A Tale of Endless Resistance and Resilience
👤 Şencan, Selin 📅 2026-10-19
Reading Samad Behrangi’s The Little Black Fish (1968) as a children’s story of political and philosophical significance, this article examines how the fish’s movement from the pond toward the sea reconfigures relations among space, authority, and agency. Drawing on Beauvais’s distinction between adult authority and child might, the article argues that resistance develops as the younger subject tests inherited knowledge across changing spatial and social conditions. The pond establishes an enclosed order sustained by inherited knowledge and collective conformity, whereas the fish’s departure brings it into encounters that alter its understanding and capacity to act. The sea intensifies this tension by combining expanded mobility with continuing exposure to capture and violence. The article also considers the story’s framing structure, in which the grandmother fish recounts the journey to a younger generation and one red fish remains awake thinking about the sea. This final act of transmission gives the narrative a pedagogical dimension. The article argues that The Little Black Fish presents resistance as a relational process produced through questioning, encounter, and collective action, while resilience emerges through the capacity to retain, adapt, and reactivate knowledge across changing spatial conditions. Beauvais’s account of authority and child might consequently intersects with the tale’s changing geographies where agency develops through what the younger subject learns, carries forward, and makes usable under circumstances that could not have been anticipated in advance.
