鱼类毒理、养殖技术与渔业社会生态研究热点

本周研究概览

本周共检索到7篇渔业相关论文，研究主题较为分散，主要涵盖水产贸易与保护政策、鱼类生活史特征、贝类养殖与食品安全、渔业废弃物资源化利用以及水产品抗菌保鲜等方向。整体来看，本周研究呈现出明显的应用导向特征，涉及渔业管理政策辩论、养殖产业实践优化及水产品加工增值等现实议题；同时，宏观生态学视角下的鱼类生活史研究亦有所涉及。值得注意的是，本周论文多发表于应用型期刊（如 *Fishes*、*Foods*、*Energies*），缺乏来自顶级综合性期刊的突破性成果，研究整体偏向于领域内的渐进式推进。

高影响力期刊亮点

本周暂无发表在Nature、Science、PNAS、Nature Climate Change、Nature Ecology & Evolution、Nature Sustainability、Nature Communications、Science Advances等高水平期刊上的论文。

创新性研究

  1. Body size, functional form and environmental correlates of maximum longevity in marine-occurring fishes（*Journal of Fish Biology*）— 该研究基于757个海洋鱼类物种的大样本数据，系统检验了最大寿命与体型之间的关系形态，并首次将广泛的环境梯度纳入分析框架。其创新之处在于超越了传统单一体型-寿命异速关系的简单描述，揭示了环境因子对寿命变异的独立贡献，为海洋鱼类生活史进化理论提供了新的实证基础。
  1. Valorization of Fish Waste via Anaerobic Digestion: A Systematic Literature Review and Future Research Agenda（*Energies*）— 该研究采用文献计量学与系统综述相结合的方法，系统梳理了鱼类加工和养殖废弃物通过厌氧消化转化为可再生能源的研究现状。其创新点在于将分散的跨学科证据进行整合，识别了该领域的研究空白，并提出了明确的未来研究方向，对推动渔业循环经济发展具有方法论上的示范意义。
  1. An Ecotoxicological Perspective on Bivalve Safety Frameworks: Linking HAB Management and Microbiological Limits to Physiological Responses in Shellfish（*Journal of Marine Science and Engineering*）— 该综述从生态毒理学视角批判性地审视了现行贝类安全监管框架的局限性，指出当前基于急性人体毒性阈值的标准未能充分反映贝类-有害藻华（HAB）相互作用的生物学复杂性。这一跨学科整合视角为完善贝类安全评估体系提供了新的理论切入点。
  1. Synergistic Effects Between Chitooligosaccharide–Epigallocatechin Gallate Conjugate and Nisin on Inhibition of Seafood-Associated Vibrio spp., Aeromonas spp., and Shewanella spp.（*Foods*）— 该研究探索了壳寡糖-表没食子儿茶素没食子酸酯共轭物与乳酸链球菌素（Nisin）联用对海产品相关腐败菌和条件致病菌的协同抑制作用，为开发新型天然复合抗菌保鲜剂提供了实验依据，在方法上体现了天然产物化学与食品微生物学的交叉创新。

推荐阅读

  1. Body size, functional form and environmental correlates of maximum longevity in marine-occurring fishes — 大样本量、多维度分析框架为理解海洋鱼类生活史进化提供了迄今最为系统的实证证据。
  2. Valorization of Fish Waste via Anaerobic Digestion: A Systematic Literature Review and Future Research Agenda — 系统综述方法严谨，对渔业废弃物资源化领域的研究空白识别精准，具有明确的应用指导价值。
  3. An Ecotoxicological Perspective on Bivalve Safety Frameworks: Linking HAB Management and Microbiological Limits to Physiological Responses in Shellfish — 以生态毒理学视角挑战现行监管范式，对贝类安全评估体系改革具有启发性。
  4. Seasonal Growth, Reproduction, and Health of the Pacific Oysters Cultured in an Intertidal Rack System in Garorim Bay, West Coast of Korea — 提供了太平洋牡蛎在潮间带架式养殖系统中的完整季节性生物学数据，对养殖管理实践具有直接参考价值。
  5. Synergistic Effects Between Chitooligosaccharide–Epigallocatechin Gallate Conjugate and Nisin on Inhibition of Seafood-Associated Vibrio spp., Aeromonas spp., and Shewanella spp. — 天然来源复合抗菌策略为海产品保鲜技术升级提供了新思路，兼具学术价值与产业转化潜力。

Food environments shaping small fish consumption among primary school-age children in Northern Uganda
👤 Siyanga, Fridah; Andersen, Peter; Kjellevold, Marian; Koldin... 📅 2026-09-06
Abstract Small fish are increasingly recognized as affordable, nutrient-rich animal-source foods, yet little is known about their consumption among primary school-age children, particularly within home and school food environments. This study applied Turner’s food environment framework to examine factors influencing fish consumption among primary school-age children in three districts of Northern Uganda. Using a convergent parallel mixed-methods design, we combined quantitative data from a cross-sectional survey of 109 households involving 280 children with qualitative data from 16 focus group discussions with parents. Children consumed fish primarily at home, mainly as environmentally friendly processed small fish, while school meals largely comprised carbohydrate-rich, plant-based foods. Both external and personal food environment factors influenced fish consumption, but affordability emerged as the dominant driver, mediating the effects of availability, market access, convenience, and desirability. We demonstrate that applying the food environment framework provides a comprehensive understanding of how these interacting factors shape children’s fish consumption. The limited inclusion of nutrient-dense small fish in school meals represents a missed opportunity to improve dietary quality and nutrition among school-age children, with implications for nutrition-sensitive and sustainable school feeding programs in Uganda and other low- and middle-income countries.

Organ-Specific Toxicity of Lynestrenol in Mandarin Fish: Hepatic Metabolic Collapse versus Ovarian Resilience through Efflux Transporter Activation
👤 Li, Weibin; Chen, Weijian; Lin, Shengyue; Cai, Guojun; Deng,... 📅 2026-09-06
Abstract Lynestrenol (LYN), a fourth-generation synthetic progestin whose active metabolite norethisterone is frequently detected in aquatic environments, poses potential risks to fish. Using integrated multi-omics, enzymology, and histopathology, we investigated the organ-specific toxicity of chronic LYN exposure (0.01, 1 μg/L for 60 days) in female mandarin fish (Siniperca chuatsi). LYN induced severe, dose-dependent hepatic necrosis, driven by concurrent blockade of the 2-oxocarboxylic acid metabolism pathway (via idh1 suppression) and ammonia detoxification failure. Additionally, LYN triggered a multi-node blockade of the ubiquinoid antioxidant system through coordinated downregulation of hpda, nqo1, and ggcx, resulting in oxidative collapse (increased ROS, MDA, ALT; decreased T-AOC, NQO1, IDH1). In contrast, ovaries remained histologically intact with no masculinization. Ovarian resilience involved dual adaptation: recalibration of steroidogenesis (downregulation of cyp19a, hsd11b2, and cpeb2 coupled with esr1 upregulation) and, for the first time in teleost gonads under progestin exposure, upregulation of ABCC efflux transporters (abcc2/3), via l-arginine/Nrf2 signaling. These findings reveal that LYN causes catastrophic hepatic metabolic collapse while ovarian tissue maintains homeostasis through active detoxification and steroidogenic reprogramming, providing a mechanistic basis for multi-organ risk assessment of progestins.

Increasing the Survival Rate of Banggai Cardinal Fish by using the Artificial Sea Urchin method at Kilo 5 Beach, Luwuk, Banggai Regency
👤 Syakir, Mohammad; Sahli, Mohammad; Mansyur, Kasim 📅 2026-09-06
The Banggai Cardinalfish (Pterapogon kauderni) is an endemic species with high ecological and economic value, but faces the threat of population decline due to habitat degradation and fishing pressure. One important factor in the survival of this species is the availability of protective microhabitats, such as sea urchins, which serve as shelter from predators. This study aims to increase the survival rate of the Banggai Cardinalfish through the application of the Artificial Sea Urchin method introduced at the Coral Transplantation site at Kilo 5 Beach, Luwuk, Banggai Regency. The method used included the installation of 10 artificial structures resembling sea urchins at the study site, as well as monitoring the survival rate, behavior, and adaptation of the fish throughout the observation period. The results showed that the use of Artificial Sea Urchins increased the survival rate by 85%, indicated by the persistence of the number of surviving individuals and the tendency of the fish to utilize the structures as shelter. Furthermore, the artificial structures also contributed to reducing predation pressure and increasing microhabitat stability. Thus, the Artificial Sea Urchin method can be an effective alternative conservation strategy to support the sustainability of the Banggai Cardinalfish population in its natural habitat.

Sustainable Aqua-Tech: Transforming Aquaculture in Bangladesh Using Modern Technological Innovations
👤 Islam, Md. Khairul; Afrin, Rafia 📅 2026-09-06
Aquaculture in Bangladesh is primarily characterized by smallholder inland freshwater carp, pangas, and tilapia farming in ponds, as well as some shrimp and prawn farming in coastal areas. It is quite different from the large-scale offshore systems frequently discussed in global technology reviews, so there is a gap between the available innovations and what is feasible locally. This review covers technologies like IoT, Cloud computing, Artificial intelligence, Robotics, Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS), Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture (IMTA), aquaponics, and Alternative protein feeds for fish. The technologies are assessed for their suitability to the size, infrastructure, and economics of Bangladeshi aquaculture. The results indicate that the most feasible alternatives for the present are the use of low-cost sensor-based aeration, mobile and SMS management tools, small-scale biofloc-assisted RAS for hatcheries and high-value species, and plant or insect-based feed alternatives. This is supported by recent pilot projects on tilapia-catfish RAS (Bangladesh) and on IMTA prawn systems. In contrast, technologies suitable for offshore or larger-scale operations, like full-scale industrial RAS, deep-sea smart cages, and traditional seaweed-based marine IMTA, are hampered by problems of unreliable electricity supply, lack of resources for accessing capital, support services, and IMTA's inability to match the mostly freshwater, inland production in Bangladesh. Thus, technology implementation in Bangladesh must be delivered on a local level and local infrastructure rather than being blindly repeated from foreign models. This review provides a pathway for near- and long-term adoption to inform policy and investment decisions.

Transforming Bhukere Local Ecological Knowledge through Fish Puppet Theater to Strengthen Lake Sentani Biodiversity Literacy in Early Childhood Education
👤 Putri Prabu Utami; Suseno, Bayu Aji; Astuti, Retnoning Adji ... 📅 2026-09-06
Lake Sentani is a freshwater ecosystem of significant ecological and socio-cultural value to the people of Papua; however, the sustainability of its biodiversity faces pressure from habitat degradation and the presence of introduced fish species. This study aims to reconstruct ecological knowledge embedded in the bhukere practice as a source of ecological literacy, develop a bhukere-based fish puppet theater to introduce the biodiversity of Lake Sentani, and identify children's responses to this medium. The study employed the Research and Development (R&D) method with a descriptive qualitative approach, encompassing stages of exploration, design, development, implementation, evaluation, and product refinement. The research subjects consisted of 58 children aged 4–5 years from two early childhood education centers in Waena Urban Village, Jayapura City. The developed product is a puppet theater performance titled Niko and Bhukere, which integrates local fish characters, ecological narratives, music, children's songs, and bhukere props made from natural materials and inorganic waste. The results indicate that the performance achieved a positive attention level of 85.4%, an increase in biodiversity understanding from 48.3% to 88.4%, a positive affective response of 88.4%, and a conservation behavior rate of 82.8%. These findings demonstrate that bhukere based fish puppet theater holds pedagogical potential as a medium for ecological literacy that is contextual, participatory, and grounded in local culture. Integrating community ecological knowledge, Lake Sentani's biodiversity, and performing arts offers an innovative strategy for fostering ecological awareness and concern among early childhood learners.
