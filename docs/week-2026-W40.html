水产养殖减依赖与鱼类免疫遗传研究新进展

本周研究概览

本周共收录渔业相关论文10篇，研究方向涵盖水产养殖营养与饲料替代、鱼类寄生虫分类学、鱼类生殖生物学与种群遗传学、渔业可持续性评估、鱼类病毒性疾病防控以及微藻水处理技术等多个领域。整体来看，水产养殖的可持续发展是本周的核心议题，多篇论文围绕饲料替代、天然活性物质应用和水质净化展开研究。与此同时，分类学与生态学基础研究亦有新发现，包括泰国淡水鱼类寄生虫新种和台湾吻虾虎鱼隐存种的描述。研究方法上，稳定同位素分析、中性粒模糊评价框架等新方法的应用值得关注。

创新性研究

  1. β-myrcene: A nature-identical compound for sustainable aquaculture（Journal of the World Aquaculture Society）

该研究提出β-月桂烯（β-myrcene）作为一种天然等同化合物在水产养殖中的可持续应用潜力。其创新之处在于将植物源天然化合物系统引入水产养殖的病害防控与生长促进领域，为减少化学药物依赖提供了新思路，对推动绿色水产养殖具有潜在影响。

  1. Assessing the Sustainability Performance of Fishing Harbors Under Uncertainty: A Gaussian-Neutrosophic Framework（Sustainable Development）

该研究首次将高斯中性模糊集（Gaussian-Neutrosophic）框架引入渔港可持续性评估，有效处理了评价过程中的不确定性问题。其方法论创新为渔业基础设施的可持续管理提供了更稳健的决策工具，有望推广至其他渔业管理场景。

  1. A new cryptic species of Rhinogobius from northern Taiwan（Journal of Fish Biology）

该研究描述了采自台湾翡翠水库的吻虾虎鱼隐存种——*Rhinogobius feitsuiensis* sp. nov.，并对*R. similis*种复合体内的隐存多样性进行了讨论。隐存种的发现提示该物种复合体的多样性可能被严重低估，对台湾及东亚淡水鱼类生物多样性保护与分类学修订具有重要意义。

  1. Antiviral activity of chlorogenic acid against largemouth bass virus（Journal of Fish Biology）

该研究首次系统评估了绿原酸（chlorogenic acid, CGA）对大口黑鲈病毒（LMBV）的体内外抗病毒活性。将天然多酚化合物应用于鱼类病毒病防控，为水产养殖中抗病毒药物的开发提供了候选分子，也为替代传统化学抗病毒策略开辟了新路径。

  1. Identification of Microalgae, Evaluation of Water Purification Efficiency, and Optimization of Large-Scale Production Conditions Based on RAS（Plants）

该研究筛选了四种微藻对循环水养殖系统（RAS）废水中氨氮和亚硝态氮的去除能力，并优化了大规模生产条件。其创新在于将微藻水处理与RAS实际工况相结合，为养殖尾水的生物净化提供了可规模化实施的方案。

Heterojunction Engineering of POMOF and ZnO: A Promising Strategy to Boost Triethylamine Gas-Sensing Performance for Fish Freshness Evaluation
👤 Mei, Meng; Zheng, Yi; Cheng, Kaixin; Yao, Bo; Wang, Liao; Wu... 📅 2026-09-30
Abstract Triethylamine (TEA) serves as a key indicator of fish spoilage, and its accurate detection is essential for assessing fish freshness. However, existing TEA gas sensors often suffer from low sensitivity, sluggish response/recovery kinetics, and high operating temperatures. To address these limitations, this study, for the first time, integrates polyoxometalate-based metal–organic frameworks (POMOF) with ZnO via heterojunction engineering to construct a POMOF-ZnO heterojunction gas sensor. At an operating temperature of 140 °C, the sensor exhibits outstanding performance toward 10 ppm TEA, including a high response value (64.54), fast response/recovery times (49/108 s), excellent selectivity, and good long-term stability over 32 days. Material characterization reveals that the heterojunction formed between POMOF and ZnO significantly increases the number of active sites on the material surface. Furthermore, combining density functional theory (DFT) calculations, current–voltage (I–V) measurements, and in-situ Fourier transform infrared (FTIR) analysis, the underlying gas-sensing mechanism is systematically and comprehensively discussed. The practical applicability of the sensor is demonstrated by its successful detection of TEA released from crucian carp during room-temperature storage, highlighting its potential for rapid assessment of fish freshness. This work presents a novel approach for designing high-performance gas sensors based on POMOF-ZnO heterojunctions for fish freshness evaluation.

ABSTRACT The morphology of the olfactory organ of fish exhibits considerable variability, which reflects taxonomical position and also adaptations to the diverse environmental conditions in which these animals live. The findings of studies on the representative of the Mastacembelidae family, Macrognathus aculeatus , provide a further illustration of this phenomenon. This species is a carnivorous, bottom‐dwelling fish that frequently buries itself in the substrate. The olfactory organ present in M. aculeatus consists of left and right elongated canals, each with an olfactory rosette. The olfactory canal, situated posterior to the tubular incurrent nostril, is lined with non‐sensory squamous epithelium. In the subsequent broad part of the canal, the epithelium forms an olfactory rosette. The structure is composed of a small median raphe and olfactory lamellae positioned on either side of the raphe. The olfactory epithelium, which lines the olfactory lamellae, contains several types of olfactory receptor neurons (ORNs). These include ciliated and crypt ORNs, as well as ORNs with long microvilli. On the level of the excurrent nostril, there is a vent of a voluminous ethmoid accessory nasal sac. We provide a revised characterization of the olfactory organ of M. aculeatus , complementing earlier descriptions.

👤 Nakib, Nirjhar; Alam, Mohammed Shahidul; Islam, Md Rakibul; ... 📅 2026-09-29
ABSTRACT Ompok bimaculatus , a silurid catfish, is a commercially valuable and endangered species in Bangladesh. Kaptai Lake is a critical habitat for this species, yet growing market demand and ecological pressures jeopardize stock sustainability. Therefore, this study employed a length‐based approach integrating LB‐SPR and aLBI to conduct the baseline stock assessment of the species in the lake. The LB‐SPR suggests that the stock may be experiencing overfishing with a reduced spawning potential ratio ( SPR = 0.30) and unsustainable fishing pressure ( F / M = 1.32). Phytoplankton Dynamics in Contrasting Coastal Embayments of South Australia: Implications for Bivalve Aquaculture
👤 Rahman, Maziidah A.; Henderson, Shaun; van Ruth, Paul D.; Li... 📅 2026-09-29
This study investigated seasonal phytoplankton communities in two contrasting oyster-growing embayments, Coffin Bay and Franklin Harbour (Eyre Peninsula, South Australia), to assess how environmental conditions influence food availability across the full phytoplankton size spectrum. In both embayments, nutrient availability was a major driver of phytoplankton dynamics during summer, while seasonal changes in physical conditions coincided with a shift in the phytoplankton community from oligotrophic to mesotrophic assemblages. In Coffin Bay, the community was dominated by picoplankton (<2 µm), accounting for more than 50% of total biomass for over 8 months of the year. In contrast, Franklin Harbour was dominated by larger phytoplankton (>20 µm). Productivity in outer Coffin Bay was primarily supported by inflowing shelf waters, which sustained peak picoeukaryote biomass (44.27 µg C L−1), and was further enhanced by a late-summer upwelling event in the eastern Great Australian Bight. However, productivity in Franklin Harbour was primarily generated internally, with the highest picoeukaryote biomass (68.15 µg C L−1) in the southern end. This study provides new insights into phytoplankton dynamics across the full spectrum in two contrasting embayments. The findings demonstrate how variations across the phytoplankton size spectrum can inform assessments of coastal productivity and carrying capacity, with implications for the sustainable management of bivalve aquaculture in temperate coastal regions.

The Impact of Fisheries and Aquaculture Production on Carbon Emissions: The Role of Natural Resource Rents
👤 Atılgan, Dilek 📅 2026-09-28
This study investigates the long-run relationships between aquaculture production (AP), fisheries production (FP), natural resource rents (NRR), economic growth, and CO₂ emissions in China, India, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, and Malaysia over the period 1990–2021. Long-run relationships are examined using the LM bootstrap co-integration test, while the Common Correlated Effects Mean Group (CCEMG) estimator is employed for long-run coefficient estimation. The Augmented Mean Group (AMG) estimator and additional sensitivity analyses are also used to assess the robustness of the findings. The baseline CCEMG results do not reveal statistically significant panel-level effects of AP, FP, NRR, or GDP. However, the Augmented Mean Group (AMG) results indicate that AP is negatively associated with CO₂ emissions, whereas FP and GDP have positive effects, while NRR remains statistically insignificant. Country-specific estimates reveal substantial heterogeneity, with AP reducing CO₂ emissions in China, India, and Malaysia, while GDP increases emissions in India, Thailand, and Malaysia. NRR also exhibits heterogeneous effects, increasing emissions in India while reducing them in Vietnam. Sensitivity analyses further indicate that the estimated effect of FP varies across model specifications, while the main findings are not solely driven by China. Overall, the results highlight the importance of country-specific production structures, resource management practices, and economic conditions in shaping the environmental effects of aquatic food production. The findings support country-specific policies promoting energy-efficient and environmentally sustainable aquatic food systems.

Growth Performance and Relative Condition Factor of Indian Major Carps and Few Local Fish Fauna in Choral River, Indore City (M.P.), India
👤 Rajput, Resham; Wast, Nageshwar; Verma, Rohit; Kumar, Ankit 📅 2026-09-29
Length–weight relationships and condition indices were examined for 17 fish species collected from the Choral River, Indore City, Madhya Pradesh, India, during February–April 2025. Fish sampling was conducted three times in each month, and a total of 1,530 individuals were considered across the three-month period. Total length and body weight were measured, and length–weight relationships were estimated using log-transformed data. Fulton’s condition factor (K) and relative condition factor (Kₙ) were also calculated. The regression exponent (b) varied among species and months, ranging from 1.0759 in Catla catla during February to 3.8230 in Glossogobius giuris during April, indicating both negative and positive allometric growth patterns. This study aims to analyze the influence of job satisfaction and organizational commitment on Organizational Citizenship Behavior (OCB) through Employee Engagement among employees of the Maluku Province Maritime Affairs and Fisheries Service. The study used a quantitative approach with a sample of 60 employees determined through a purposive sampling technique. Data were collected through questionnaires and analyzed using the Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling (PLS-SEM) method with the help of SmartPLS. The results showed that job satisfaction had a positive and significant effect on Employee Engagement and OCB. These findings indicate that Employee Engagement has an important role in encouraging the formation of OCB behavior. Therefore, increasing job satisfaction, strengthening organizational commitment, and creating employee involvement in work need to be a concern in supporting positive behavior and employee contributions outside of formal duties.
