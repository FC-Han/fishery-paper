创新性研究

  1. Interaction Mechanism Between Grass Carp Fish Maw Collagen and Heavy-Metal Ions（*International Journal of Molecular Sciences*）

该研究首次系统表征了草鱼鱼鳔胶原蛋白（热溶性胶原HSC及另一组分）与常见重金属离子的结合行为与分子机制，填补了鱼鳔胶原-金属离子互作分子层面认识的空白。其创新点在于将水产品加工副产物的安全性问题与蛋白质-金属配位化学相结合，为鱼鳔类产品的重金属风险评估和脱除工艺开发提供了分子基础。

  1. Ageing imprecision compounds the effects of fishing selectivity, increasing bias in estimates of growth parameters（*Journal of Fish Biology*）

该研究的创新之处在于首次将 ageing error（年龄鉴定误差）与 fishing selectivity（捕捞选择性）的联合效应纳入生长参数估计偏差的量化框架，而非此前惯常的独立分析。这一发现对渔业资源评估中生长参数的可靠性具有直接影响，提示管理者在数据质量欠佳的情境下需重新审视基于长度频率的评估结论。

  1. A new species of *Pimelodella* from the upper Bermejo basin（*Journal of Fish Biology*）

该文以形态学与遗传数据相结合的方式描述并命名了Bermejo流域上游的*Pimelodella*属新种，以椎骨数目（37–39）和短脂鳍基等特征与同属多数物种相区分。其意义在于丰富了La Plata basin鱼类区系记录，并为该属的系统分类与生物地理学研究提供了新的分类学依据。

  1. Fished to the Edge: Severe Overfishing Threatens Fish Stocks in Tanzania's Saline Rift Valley Lakes（*Fisheries Management and Ecology*）

该研究对坦桑尼亚Kitangiri和Singida两个盐碱裂谷湖的鱼类种群状况进行了系统评估，基于渔获量、物种组成和单位努力渔获量等指标揭示了过度捕捞的严重程度。其价值在于为数据匮乏的内陆小型渔业提供了可操作的资源状态诊断框架，对东非裂谷湖区渔业管理具有直接警示意义。

Design/Methodology/Approach: The study adopts an ex-post facto research design and relies on annual time-series secondary data covering the period 1970–2024 in Nigeria. 👤 Tsegaye, Birtukan; Mingist, Minwyelet; Getahun, Abebe; Mengi... 📅 2026-11-16
ABSTRACT Lake Tana, Ethiopia, harbours the world's only remaining cyprinid species flock, making it globally significant for conservation. Yet, these resources face mounting anthropogenic pressures from overfishing and land use changes. We assessed fish assemblage structure and seasonal dynamics in the Gulf of Lake Tana from September 2024 to August 2025 and land use land cover (LULC) changes from 2000 to 2025 using Landsat and Sentinel imagery. Fish specimens were sampled monthly from fishers at landing sites employing gillnets of varying mesh sizes. The specimens were identified to species level and analysed using Shannon diversity ( H ′), evenness ( J ), dominance ( C ) and Index of Relative Importance (%IRI). Exploratory multivariate analyses (non‐metric multidimensional scaling [NMDS] and principal component analysis [PCA] of four seasonal aggregates) were used to describe seasonal assemblage patterns. A total of 2764 specimens representing 18 species were recorded, dominated by endemic Labeobarbus spp. (81.8%), followed by Oreochromis niloticus (11.9%) and Clarias gariepinus (6.3%). Diversity was moderate ( H ′ = 2.60) and differed significantly among four hydrological seasons (individual‐based permutation test, 9999 permutations: p = 0.0024), being highest in the dry season ( H ′ = 2.596). After Bonferroni correction, dry‐season H ′ differed significantly from pre‐rainy (Hutcheson t ‐test, t = 3.84, adjusted p = 0.0006) and post‐rainy H ′ ( p = 0.036), but not from rainy‐season H ′ (adjusted p = 0.066). Evenness was high in all seasons ( J = 0.87–0.95). Sex ratios were female‐biased in the catches of 12 of 18 species ( χ 2 , p < 0.001). Between 2000 and 2025, cropland and settlements expanded to nearly 60% of the mapped area, whereas wetlands declined by 92.7%. Because, fish data cover a single year and no environmental covariates were measured, these LULC trends are presented only as landscape context that is associated with the current assemblage composition. The findings provide baseline data for further studies, testing these associations directly, and support the need for watershed rehabilitation, buffer zone protection and sustained fisheries monitoring to protect the lake's globally unique cyprinid flock.

👤 Ukagwu, John I.; Nwosu, Chijioke Ralph; Nkulo, Uzoma U.; Eze... 📅 2026-11-16
This study assessed fish species composition, relative abundance and diversity in Nworie River, Imo State, Nigeria, using fish-catch records obtained between January and August 2026. The assessment aimed to establish a baseline for subsequent biodiversity monitoring and fisheries management. Records were drawn from three sampling stations representing the upstream (A), midstream (B) and downstream (C) sections of the river. Specimens were collected fortnightly using a gill net and a dugout canoe, and records were pooled across stations. The findings indicate unequal numerical representation among the recorded species and provide an initial fish assemblage inventory for Nworie River. Because the analysis concerns pooled catch records, the estimates should not be interpreted as absolute population abundances. Continued taxonomic verification and effort-standardised monitoring are recommended.

👤 Breau, Cindy; Chaput, Gérald J; Friedland, Kevin D. 📅 2026-11-16
Across its range, the survival and maturation dynamics of Atlantic salmon (Salmo salar) are dependent on growth, energy reserves, and predation pressure. This study examined growth of one-sea-winter (1SW) and two-sea-winter (2SW) salmon using archived scales collected from the Miramichi River, Canada, spanning 59 smolt migration years (1958-2016). Fork length and nearly all scale-derived growth metrics exhibited significant long-term linear trends, however, no evidence was found for critical periods of reduced growth. However, freezing conditions may influence ice crystal formation and cellular damage, affecting nutrient retention. Objectives To assess the impact of deep-freezing on the nutrient profile of the most commonly consumed fish and seafood in the Italian population, including Cod, Hake, Gilthead Seabream, Plaice, Salmon, Seabass, Octopus and Shrimp. Methods Studies evaluating changes in proteins, amino acids, lipids, fatty acids, vitamins, and minerals during deep-frozen storage, as well as differences in fresh vs deep-frozen fish were evaluated. Results Thirteen studies were included. Total protein content was largely preserved, although reductions in protein solubility and myofibrillar functionality were reported at higher temperatures or after prolonged storage. Total lipid content was generally stable, while fatty acid composition showed variable changes, with occasional decreases in polyunsaturated fatty acids over time. Vitamin retention was more heterogeneous, with water-soluble vitamins appearing more susceptible to degradation, than fat-soluble ones. No changes in mineral content were assessed. Conclusions Despite methodological heterogeneity, the evidence suggests that rapid deep-freezing and lower storage temperatures better preserve nutritional quality. Further standardized studies are needed to improve comparability across species and storage conditions.

In contrast to terrestrial vertebrates, aquatic organisms acquire iron not only from dietary sources but also directly from the surrounding water through the gills. This dual acquisition pathway has driven unique regulatory adaptations of iron homeostasis, maintained through tightly coordinated mechanisms of absorption, transport, and storage. Disruption of these mechanisms, either by deficiency or overload, can impair growth, cause anemia, promote oxidative stress, and aggravate metabolic disorders. This review synthesizes current knowledge of iron uptake routes, systemic regulation, storage, and recycling in major aquaculture species. In addition, it provides an overview of dietary iron requirements, iron deficiency and overload, and the effects of feed composition, mineral sources, functional additives and environmental factors on iron bioavailability. Finally, the review discusses ferroptosis, an iron‐dependent form of programmed cell death associated with lipid peroxidation, and highlights critical knowledge gaps and research priorities for improving iron nutrition and metabolic health in aquaculture species.
