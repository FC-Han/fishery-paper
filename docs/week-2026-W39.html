水产养殖多元创新：从病害防控到可持续发展

本周研究概览

本周共收录渔业相关论文15篇，研究主题涵盖水产养殖技术优化、渔业资源评估、鱼类生物学与生态学、水产品安全及人工智能应用等多个方向。从期刊分布来看，*Aquaculture, Fish and Fisheries* 和 *Journal of Fish Biology* 各发表3篇，*Problems of Fisheries* 发表4篇，其余散见于 *Aquaculture*、*Microorganisms* 等期刊。整体研究趋势呈现两个特征：一是水产养殖的可持续性议题持续升温，涉及替代蛋白源开发、养殖密度优化和季节特异性饲料设计；二是区域性渔业资源评估与生物学研究仍占重要地位，尤其以俄罗斯和韩国水域的种群研究为代表。

高影响力期刊亮点

本周暂无发表在 Nature、Science、PNAS 等上述高影响力期刊上的论文。

创新性研究

  1. Responsible AI Guardrails in *Aquaculture, Fish and Fisheries*: Enabling Innovation With Accountability and Integrity — 该文探讨了在水产养殖与渔业研究中部署负责任人工智能的治理框架，提出在创新与学术诚信之间建立平衡的"护栏"机制。这是渔业领域少见的AI伦理与治理讨论，对规范AI辅助研究具有前瞻性意义。
  1. Atlantic salmon (*Salmo salar*) aquaculture in a changing climate: Quantitative insights from industry records and climate projections — 该研究将产业实际记录与降尺度气候模型相结合，定量评估气候变化对北大西洋鲑养殖的影响。其创新在于利用卫星海表温度数据进行统计降尺度，为养殖业的气候适应性规划提供了可操作的量化依据。
  1. The Pacific seaweed pipefish *Syngnathus schlegeli* exhibits exceptionally high levels of polygynandry among fishes with paternal egg care — 该研究发现海龙科鱼类 *S. schlegeli* 具有鱼类父系抚育中极为罕见的高水平一雌多雄一雄多雌制（polygynandry），对理解性选择与交配制度演化具有重要意义。
  1. Development of a Multiplex Quantitative PCR Assay for Simultaneous Detection of Multiple *Vibrio* Species in Aquaculture Samples — 该研究开发了可同时检测多种弧菌的多重qPCR方法，解决了水产养殖中弧菌病原快速诊断的技术瓶颈，对疾病监测和防控具有直接应用价值。
  1. Towards Season-Specific Feeds: Maximising Protein Digestibility and Reducing Waste in Hybrid Abalone Aquaculture — 该研究提出按季节水温变化设计差异化饲料的策略，以最大化杂交鲍的蛋白质消化率并减少废物排放，体现了精准营养与环境保护相结合的理念。

Beyond Probiotics: Postbiotics for Healthy Aquaculture
👤 Chikindas, Michael Leonidas; Xu, Delin; Popov, Igor V.; Carn... 📅 2026-09-27
ABSTRACT With a growing global population and strict restrictions on traditional antibiotics, sustainable fish health management is crucial for aquaculture. While live probiotics are popular alternatives, their real‐world effectiveness remains highly controversial. Variable effectiveness results from complex interactions with different fish species, differences in fish gastrointestinal anatomy, and variations in environmental parameters such as salinity, pH, and temperature. Furthermore, live probiotics pose risks of ecological disturbance and horizontal gene transfer of antimicrobial resistance. This critical review assesses these limitations and proposes postbiotics, preparations of inanimate microorganisms and their metabolites, as a more stable, controlled, and biosafe formulation. Postbiotics offer significant advantages during industrial aquafeed production and storage. However, their practical implementation is currently hindered by vague terminology and legislative gaps. To overcome these obstacles, this review introduces an innovative modular analytical model utilizing specific chemical biomarkers (such as short‐chain fatty acids, bacteriocins, and lipoteichoic acids). Implementing these matrix‐matched, multi‐module quality control panels to verify molecular safety, identity, and efficacy is critical to standardizing next‐generation “precision postbiotics” for sustainable aquaculture development.

Lernaea cyprinacea is the parasitic crustacean causative of lernaeosis in freshwater fish, a disease widespread globally. Management of these infestations is of utmost importance to prevent disease outbreaks and avoid financial losses for aquaculture. Environmental DNA for detecting marine fish species: testing the volume of sampled water, markers and primers in a public oceanarium
👤 Ferreira, André O.; Machado, Cláudia; Barroso, Cristina; Bay... 📅 2026-09-27
To maximize the efficiency of marine fish detection using environmental DNA (eDNA) metabarcoding, methodological factors such as PCR amplification bias and sampled water volume must be carefully considered. In this study, we evaluated the influence of different genetic markers (COI, 12S rRNA, and 16S rRNA) and water sample volumes (0.5, 1.0, 2.5, 5.0, and 10 L) on eDNA-based detection of 60 marine fish species maintained at the Oceanário de Lisboa. Five primer sets were used to generate amplicon libraries from triplicate water samples per tested volume. Overall, the combined use of all markers enabled the detection of 81.7% of the fish species present in the tank (n=49), as well as 37 additional exogenous taxa. Detection success varied among primer sets, ranging from 31.7% for COI FishATL_Cocktail1 to 73.3% for Fish16S. When accounting for reference sequence availability, relative detection success reached 82.4% for 12S and 77.2% for 16S. Increasing water volume consistently led to higher species detection across all markers, with detection percentages approaching saturation at the highest volumes tested. When pooling all markers, up to 76.7% of the species present were detected using a water volume of 5 L. These results highlight the limitations of relying on a single genetic marker and demonstrate the strong influence of sampling volume on detection efficiency, a factor likely to be even more critical in open marine environments.

Tenebrio molitor larvae meal as fish meal alternative: impacts in juvenile narrow-clawed crayfish (Pontastacus leptodactylus)
👤 Uzunmehmetoğlu, Oğuz Yaşar; Bahadır Koca, Seval; Özmen, Özle... 📅 2026-09-27
A 60-day trial examined replacing fish meal (FM) protein with defatted Tenebrio molitor (TM) larvae meal in diets for juvenile narrow-clawed crayfish (Pontastacus leptodactylus). Six isonitrogenous, isoenergetic diets with 0–100% FM protein replacement were tested on 600 stage II juveniles (four replicates of 25 per treatment).Growth performance was similar between the control and 40% TM groups (final weight and SGR did not differ significantly, p&gt;0.05), but replacement levels above 40% significantly reduced growth and survival, with the 100% TM group showing the lowest final weight and survival (48%). Molting frequency and cheliped injury did not differ across treatments. Histological analysis showed normal hepatopancreas and intestine in the control and 20% TM groups. At ≥40% TM inclusion, hepatopancreatic damage (pigment deposits, elevated B-cell counts, degeneration, necrosis) and progressive shortening of intestinal villi were observed, along with luminal content stasis. Overall, up to 20% FM replacement with TM larvae meal can be used safely without harming growth or gut/hepatopancreas health, while higher inclusion levels risk long-term digestive tissue damage.

The sector faces ongoing challenges, including high energy consumption, land‐use conflicts, and unreliable electricity supply, all of which impede its sustainable development and pose broader risks to both human survival and long‐term ecological balance. The quest for efficient solar energy utilization has led to the emergence of aquavoltaics, a novel integrated model that combines photovoltaic technology with aquaculture. Aquavoltaics offers a promising solution to mitigate climate change while addressing the growing global demands for food and energy. This review paper explores the current state of aquaculture production and energy consumption, detailing the functionality of aquavoltaic systems, their impact on water quality parameters, and their influence on the productivity of cultured organisms. Additionally, the paper discusses how aquavoltaics fulfill the water‐energy‐food nexus, offering insights into improving aquaculture production and clean energy. Finally, it addresses the sustainability aspects, challenges, and future prospects of aquavoltaic systems, highlighting their potential role in advancing sustainable development.

Rheological, physicochemical, and structural properties of fish gelatin/carboxymethyl cellulose/Persian gum ternary edible films
👤 Hosseini, Seyed Fakhreddin; Allahyari, Maryam; Gómez-Guillén... 📅 2026-09-27
Abstract This study explored the modulation of fish gelatin (FG)/carboxymethyl cellulose (CMC) edible films using Persian gum (PG), focusing on structure-rheology-function relationships and physicochemical performance. Ternary FG/CMC/PG films with mass ratios of 60/40/0, 54/36/10, 48/32/20, 42/28/30, and 36/24/40 were prepared by solution casting. Rheological analysis of film-forming solutions revealed shear-thinning behavior across all formulations, with 10% PG increasing viscosity, possibly because of favorable polymer associations and entanglements, whereas higher PG concentrations (20–40%) decreased viscosity, possibly reflecting concentration-dependent network rearrangement and increasing heterogeneity. Dynamic oscillatory measurements showed that incorporation of PG shifted the viscoelastic response toward a more elastic, solid-like network, suggesting enhanced interactions between PG and FG/CMC chains. Mechanical testing indicated that increasing PG content decreased tensile strength and elongation-at-break, while water vapor permeability increased from 0.74 to 0.95 g mm/kPa h m 2 , and solubility decreased by up to 41% relative to the control, reflecting changes in polymer interactions and film structure. Optical analyses demonstrated that PG improved film transparency. Structural characterization through Fourier-transform infrared (FT-IR) spectroscopy, X-ray diffraction (XRD), and differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) analyses provided evidence of intermolecular interactions and compositional effects on molecular organization, while SEM and AFM observations revealed increasing morphological heterogeneity at higher PG concentrations, suggesting possible microphase separation. Overall, the FG/CMC/PG ternary films developed in this study exhibited tunable functional properties and demonstrated the potential of Persian gum as a natural modifier for developing biodegradable films with potential suitability for food-packaging applications.
