渔业资源保护与养殖技术创新

本周研究概览

本周共收录渔业相关论文27篇，研究覆盖面较为广泛，涉及水产养殖技术与管理、鱼类生理与遗传、渔业生态与栖息地、鱼类病害与食品安全、以及渔业社会经济等多个方向。从研究区域看，论文来自中国、韩国、印度尼西亚、尼日利亚、罗马尼亚、希腊、菲律宾、阿根廷等多个国家和地区，呈现出明显的国际化特征。整体研究趋势上，水产养殖的可持续性（替代饲料、生物材料利用、病害防控）与渔业生态连通性（eDNA、栖息地评估）构成本周的两大主线，同时应用型社区研究和技术方法创新也占有一定比例。

高影响力期刊亮点

本周有两篇论文发表于 *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*（PNAS），值得重点关注。

Sub-ice thermal gradients strongly influence winter depth use of an iconic cold-water fish — 该研究聚焦二季混合湖（dimictic lake）冬季逆温分层条件下鱼类的深度利用行为，弥补了以往研究偏重夏季热分层的不足。研究揭示了冰下热梯度对冷水性鱼类冬季栖息地选择的强烈影响，对理解气候变化背景下湖泊鱼类越冬生态具有重要意义。

Rapid vaterite-mediated shell repair for acid-induced damage in a deep-sea mussel — 该研究通过原位实验报道了深海贻贝以球霰石（vaterite）为介导的快速壳修复过程，深化了对深海钙化生物生物矿化分子机制的认识。其发现对理解海洋酸化背景下深海贝类的适应能力具有潜在价值。

  1. eDNA metabarcoding and random forest identify super-connector fish species across mangrove–seagrass–coral reef continuums in the South China Sea（*Frontiers in Ecology and Evolution*）— 将eDNA宏条形码与随机森林机器学习相结合，识别跨红树林—海草—珊瑚礁连续体的"超级连接者"鱼类物种。方法学上把分子生态学与数据驱动模型融合，为热带海景生态连通性研究提供了可推广的分析框架。
  1. The Wearing Layer Influences Long-Term Material Loss from Composite Floats for Oyster Aquaculture（*Aquaculture Journal*）— 针对牡蛎养殖中发泡聚苯乙烯浮球作为海洋微塑料来源的问题，采用加速风化实验对八种候选材料的长期质量损失进行定量比较。该研究填补了替代材料认证标准缺失的空白，对水产养殖塑料污染的源头治理具有直接指导意义。

  1. Oceanic spawning migration of tropical Indian Ocean eels from Madagascar（*Marine Ecology Progress Series*）— 揭示了马达加斯加热带印度洋鳗鲡的海洋产卵洄游，拓展了热带鳗鲡生活史认知的地理范围。对印度洋鳗鲡资源管理与保护策略制定具有基础性意义。
  1. Phytobiotics from the Lamiaceae, Asteraceae, and Fabaceae families: Mechanisms, efficacy, and emerging nanotechnological applications in sustainable aquaculture（*Veterinary Research Communications*）— 系统综述了三大植物科植物源生物制剂在水产养殖中的作用机制与纳米技术应用前景。将传统植物提取物与纳米递送技术相衔接，为抗生素替代和可持续病害防控提供了新思路。

Determining the Aquaculture Potential of Campus Ponds Based on Water Quality Parameters for Nile Tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus) Farming
👤 Khairunnisa, Eka Nurriza; Wahyu, Ilham; Horisanto, Abraham; ... 📅 2026-11-09
Water quality largely determines the success of Nile tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus) cultivation. This study assessed whether two campus ponds at Politeknik Negeri Sriwijaya Banyuasin, Indonesia, are suitable for tilapia culture, using physical and chemical water-quality parameters and a descriptive quantitative design. Water was sampled at three stations per pond (inlet, center, outlet) on two occasions, with duplicate measurements. We reported temperature, total dissolved solids (TDS), dissolved oxygen (DO), pH, ammonia, phosphate, biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), and chemical oxygen demand (COD). Mean temperature was 32.00 ± 0.43 °C in Pond 1 and 32.42 ± 0.51 °C in Pond 2, and DO was relatively high (6.53 ± 0.19 and 6.55 ± 0.20 mg/L). TDS was low (26.42 ± 2.07 and 30.50 ± 1.68 mg/L). Mean pH was slightly acidic (6.09 ± 0.14 and 6.15 ± 0.14): below the 6.5–8.5 range used for tilapia culture, but inside the 6–9 range of Class II in Government Regulation No. 22/2021. Ammonia (0.48 ± 0.06 and 0.41 ± 0.10 mg/L), phosphate (1.25 ± 0.08 and 1.36 ± 0.09 mg/L), BOD (8.84 ± 1.44 and 10.93 ± 2.79 mg/L), and COD (29.00 ± 6.64 and 35.67 ± 9.11 mg/L) exceeded the comparison values used. Both ponds therefore offer supportive oxygen conditions but are constrained by organic loading and nutrient enrichment. They should be regarded as usable only after water-quality management and confirmatory monitoring, including dawn-time DO, nitrite, alkalinity, and the analytical basis of ammonia and phosphate. The Wearing Layer Influences Long-Term Material Loss from Composite Floats for Oyster Aquaculture: An Accelerated-Weathering Comparison of Eight Candidate Materials
👤 Mun, So Youn; Jang, Pilseong; Jang, Jun Hwan 📅 2026-11-09
Expanded polystyrene floats have been identified as a significant source of marine microplastic in oyster aquaculture, yet no certification criterion assesses the material that the certified alternatives shed. Gravimetric mass loss, normalised to a common exposed area of 7200 mm2, is reported under accredited accelerated weathering to KS F 2274 for eight float materials: expanded polystyrene at three densities, expanded polypropylene, three injection-moulded polymers, and a continuous-fibre basalt-reinforced thermoplastic composite. Specimens were dried to constant mass before weighing, so the quantity measured is a net change: an upper bound on particulate release, not a measure of it. Mass exceeded the initial value in all three stabilised expanded polypropylene specimens. At 3000 h the loss spanned a factor of 54, and every foamed material exceeded every non-foamed one, while tripling the density of expanded polystyrene reduced loss by only 25% (n = 3; p < 0.0001 across the foamed groups). For ten injection-moulded grades the accelerated ranking reproduced that of 2.5 years outdoors (Spearman ρ = 0.89), though the ratio varied from 0.4 to 6.3. A wear model indicates that the pure-polymer barrier providing water tightness contains 905–1180 g L−1 of polymer against 578 g L−1 for the laminate beneath, so the wearing layer, not the bulk material, may govern release in service.

Evaluation of Semen Quality Indices in Cultured and Wild Broodstock of Clarias gariepinus: Implications for Aquaculture Reproduction and Broodstock Management
👤 Uruku, Ndekimbe Mamndeyati; Abutu, Agnes; Uruku, Ndenyangtse... 📅 2026-11-09
African catfish (Clarias gariepinus) is an economically important aquaculture species in Nigeria, and sustainable hatchery production depends partly on the availability of suitable male broodstock and good-quality semen. This study compared selected semen quality parameters of cultured and wild male C. gariepinus to assess differences associated with broodstock source. Six sexually mature males, comprising three (3) cultured and three (3) wild individuals, were examined. Semen was obtained through testicular extraction following dissection. The evaluated parameters included gonad weight, gonadosomatic index (GSI), milt volume, sperm motility, sperm concentration, sperm viability, sperm morphology, and semen pH. Descriptive comparisons indicated that wild broodstock had higher mean gonad weight (5.33 ± 1.20 g), milt volume (2.72 ± 0.10 mL), and sperm concentration (5.82 ± 0.23 × 10¹⁰ sperm cells mL⁻¹) than cultured broodstock, which recorded 3.50 ± 0.76 g, 2.58 ± 0.17 mL, and 5.56 ± 0.11 × 10¹⁰ sperm cells mL⁻¹, respectively. The percentage of active spermatozoa was also numerically higher in wild fish (69.17 ± 7.77%) than in cultured fish (63.33 ± 14.00%). Semen pH was 7.00 in both groups. Wild broodstock recorded a higher mean proportion of morphologically normal spermatozoa (88.00 ± 2.16%) than cultured broodstock (76.80 ± 12.95%). The reported statistical analysis, however, indicated no significant differences between the groups (P > 0.05). Qualitative characteristics, including semen colour, viscosity, and liquefaction time, varied between the two sources. These findings indicate that both cultured and wild broodstock produced measurable quantities of testicular semen with broadly comparable characteristics under the conditions investigated. Further studies involving larger numbers of broodstock and controlled fertilisation trials are necessary to determine whether the observed differences influence fertilisation success, hatchability, and larval performance. This study provides preliminary information that may contribute to broodstock evaluation and management in African catfish aquaculture.

Macrobenthic fauna associated with maërl beds near aquaculture installations in the north-eastern Corinthian Gulf, Greece, with remarks on two taxa
👤 Chatzigeorgiou, Georgios; Lampa, Maria; Rallis, Ioannis; Mai... 📅 2026-11-09
Maërl beds, also known as rhodolith beds, are biogenic benthic habitats formed by unattached coralline algae and support diverse macrobenthic assemblages. In the Mediterranean, especially its eastern basin, information on their distribution, ecological condition and associated fauna remains limited. Maërl beds are sensitive to physical disturbance, sedimentation and organic enrichment, including pressures associated with aquaculture activities. This data paper describes the macrobenthic fauna associated with maërl beds located near and further from finfish farms in the Traxilos area of the north-eastern Corinthian Gulf, Greece. The dataset comprises eight sampling events and 328 abundance-based occurrence records representing 6,410 individuals. The paper also provides morphological notes on two polychaete taxa, Syllis nigricirris and the Neanthioides acuminata species complex, documenting variation in diagnostically relevant characters.

Dopaminergic stimulation of intestinal maltase activity in the freshwater fish Oligosarcus jenynsii
👤 Albanesi, Camila; Domanski, Dardo; Michiels, Maria Soledad 📅 2026-11-09
Intestinal disaccharidases are key determinants of carbohydrate assimilation in fish. Here, we characterized maltase and sucrase activity in juveniles of Oligosarcus jenynsii (Günther, 1864) and evaluated the modulation of maltase activity by dopamine. Maltase activity was detected across a broad pH (6.0–8.0) and temperature (4–45 °C) range and followed Michaelis–Menten kinetics (Km :1.22 mM), whereas sucrase activity was not detected. In vitro assays showed that dopamine significantly stimulated maltase activity in a concentration-dependent manner, with increases at 10-4 and 10-3 M. This stimulatory effect was not observed in the presence of the D1 receptor antagonist SCH-23390 or the D2 receptor antagonist chlorpromazine. These results demonstrate that intestinal maltase activity is responsive to dopaminergic stimulation under in vitro conditions and suggest the participation of dopaminergic pathways in this response. The physiological significance of this modulation in vivo remains to be established. These findings contribute to the understanding of the biochemical regulation of intestinal disaccharidases in a freshwater fish species.

Comparative Analysis of the Economic Feasibility of Using Extruded Pellets and Raw Fish-Based Moist Pellets as Aquafeed in Olive Flounder (Paralichthys olivaceus) Aquaculture in South Korea
👤 Kim, Nam-Lee; Hur, Sang Woo; Park, Kyoung-Il; Jeong, Seong-M... 📅 2026-11-09
South Korea’s olive flounder aquaculture industry predominantly relies on raw fish-based moist pellets (MP), raising concerns regarding marine resource use and environmental sustainability. This study compared the growth performance and economic feasibility of extruded pellets (EP) and MP through a 12-month feeding trial conducted at two commercial olive flounder farms in Wando, South Korea. At each farm, three replicate tanks were assigned to each dietary treatment. Economic feasibility was assessed using net present value (NPV), internal rate of return (IRR), and sensitivity analyses of key production and cost variables. Final body weight and survival rate were significantly higher in the MP groups than in the EP groups at both farms. In the baseline analysis without government subsidies, Farm A recorded an NPV of USD 793,446 and an IRR of 14.1% for EP, compared with USD 2,244,588 and 28.8% for MP. At Farm B, the corresponding values were USD 705,580 and 13.1% for EP, compared with USD 2,836,482 and 34.3% for MP. Although both feeding systems were economically feasible, MP demonstrated greater economic returns, primarily due to higher growth performance and lower feed costs. These findings highlight the need to improve EP performance and cost-effectiveness while considering the long-term sustainability of aquaculture feeding strategies.

eDNA metabarcoding and random forest identify super-connector fish species across mangrove–seagrass–coral reef continuums in the South China Sea
👤 Liu, Chunchun; Wu, Gongguo; Zou, Chujun; Wu, Donghai; Xie, P... 📅 2026-11-09
Background Tropical coastal seascapes rely on ecological connectivity between mangrove, seagrass, and coral reef habitats to sustain fish biodiversity and fisheries productivity. However, identifying key fish species that facilitate cross-habitat energy flows remains challenging due to limitations of traditional survey methods in turbid, structurally complex environments. Methods We integrated environmental DNA (eDNA) metabarcoding with Random Forest (RF) machine learning to map fish assemblages across a mangrove-seagrass-coral reef continuum in Hainan Island, South China Sea. Results We detected 234 fish species across 78 families - a 4.03-fold increase over conventional gillnet surveys (58 species, paired t-test, t = 11.34, p &lt; 0.001). Using RF classification, we identified 23 "connector species" with high cross-habitat fidelity, of which three super-connectors - Lutjanus russellii , Siganus fuscescens , and Gerres oyena - accounted for 47% of cross-habitat network flow. Simulated removal of these three species reduced overall network connectivity by 68%. The RF model achieved 90.7% accuracy (Kappa = 0.86) in predicting habitat occurrence from eDNA data. Variable importance analysis revealed a salinity threshold at 28 PSU and a distance-to-reef threshold at 2.5 km as primary drivers of community turnover. Our bipartite network analysis showed significant nestedness (NODF = 47.3, p &lt; 0.001), indicating that generalist species disproportionately maintain seascape connectivity. Discussion These findings demonstrate that integrating eDNA with machine learning provides a scalable, high-resolution toolkit for defining functional connectivity and prioritizing species for ecosystem-based fisheries management in the Anthropocene.
