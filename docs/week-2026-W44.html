本周研究概览

本周共收录8篇渔业相关论文，研究方向涵盖渔业治理与政策（采购问责、鲑鱼养殖标签与追溯）、渔业生态学（温盐锋对饵料鱼营养生态的影响、鳟鱼捕获放流的能量代价）、水产养殖工程与微生物学（RAS生物滤器低温运行、鱼类加工废弃物堆肥）、水产病害快速检测（*Perkinsus olseni* RPA-PfAgo检测）以及海洋生物技术（深度学习辅助抗菌肽发现）。整体来看，本周论文以应用导向为主，治理法规类与病害检测类研究占比偏高，基础生态学研究相对有限；跨学科方法（深度学习、等温扩增）向水产领域的渗透是值得关注的趋势。本周无高影响力综合期刊论文发表。

The influence of accountability on procurement performance: a case study ministry of blue economy and fisheries Zanzibar
👤 Manzi, Abdillah; Sama, Hamisi 📅 2026-10-26
Accountability is a foundational pillar of procurement governance, yet quantitative evidence on how accountability mechanisms specifically shape procurement outcomes within individual government ministries in Zanzibar remains scarce. This study examined the influence of accountability on procurement performance at the Ministry of Blue Economy and Fisheries (MoBEF), Zanzibar. Guided by Institutional Theory and attached in a positivist research philosophy, study adopted a descriptive and explanatory quantitative design where a structured, five-point Likert-scale questionnaire was administered to 85 respondents drawn from a target population of 108 procurement officers, tender board members, finance officers, user department representatives, internal auditors, and registered suppliers, selected through stratified random sampling using Yamane’s (1967) formula. Data were analyzed descriptively and inferentially using IBM SPSS version 23, incorporating reliability testing, correlation, and multiple linear regressions. Descriptive results revealed that accountability practices were generally well established at MoBEF, with mean scores ranging from 3.90 to 4.12. Review and approval procedures and documentation practices attracted the strongest endorsement, while disciplinary enforcement for procurement irregularities recorded both the lowest mean (M = 3.90) and the greatest variability (SD = 0.89), indicating a structural enforcement gap within the Ministry’s accountability system. Multiple regression analysis confirmed that accountability significantly and positively predicted procurement performance (β = 0.27, SE = 0.07, t = 3.57, p = .001), alongside fairness (β = 0.31, p < .001) and transparency (β = 0.29, p = .002); the overall model was statistically significant (F(3, 81) = 28.45, p < .001, R² = 0.520). The null hypothesis was rejected. These findings confirm that while formal accountability structures are well established within MoBEF, inconsistent disciplinary enforcement limits their full effect on procurement performance. The study recommends strengthening audit follow-through, enforcing consistent sanctions for non-compliance, and institutionalizing structured monitoring systems.

Comparison of three sperm-FISH protocols to optimize population-scale screening in cattle
👤 Kowal-Mierzwa, Wiktoria; Parma, Pietro; Battelli, Marco; Ian... 📅 2026-10-26
Abstract Fluorescence in situ hybridization (FISH) is widely used in chromosome structure and organization studies, playing a key role in diagnosing chromosomal aberrations in somatic and germ cells. A critical step in preparing spermatozoa for sperm-FISH analysis is effective nuclear chromatin decondensation. Sperm chromatin is highly condensed, posing a barrier to the proper hybridization of molecular probes. Insufficient or excessive decondensation can result in unclear fluorescent signals, reducing the reliability of analysis. Since optimizing decondensation is essential for achieving precise and reproducible results, this study aimed to optimize chromatin decondensation in domestic cattle spermatozoa using three different sperm-FISH protocols, to identify a protocol suitable for future population-scale sperm-FISH analyses. The research material consisted of semen from 10 bulls obtained in collaboration with the Malopolska Biotechnic Center in Krasne. Three separate protocols were applied: Protocol 1 involved decondensation in 1 M DTT in PBS; Protocol 2 used a papain–DTT solution; and Protocol 3 involved treatment with a 3 M NaOH solution. All three protocols resulted in a percentage of properly decondensed sperm, but Protocol 3 yielded a statistically higher proportion of this type. It provided well-decondensed sperm chromatin, clear fluorescent signals specific to the studied chromosomes, and allowed reliable detection of normal and abnormal signals. Alkaline denaturation may provide more stable chromatin decondensation in bovine spermatozoa, thereby improving hybridization efficiency and signal clarity.

Photovoltaic Shading Alters Water Quality and Sustains Diatom-Dominated Microbial Communities in Shrimp–Crab Aquaculture Ponds
👤 Dongping, Zhang; Shaolei, Cai; Junhao, Zhang; Dongsheng, Che... 📅 2026-10-27
Fishery–photovoltaic systems are expanding rapidly in coastal China, yet their effects on pond water quality and microbial communities remain poorly understood. We compared three PV-shaded and three unshaded (NPV) earthen ponds co-culturing Pacific white shrimp (Litopenaeus vannamei) and mud crab (Scylla paramamosain) in August and September 2025. Water quality measurements and 16S and 18S rRNA gene amplicon sequencing were conducted on 60 samples. PV ponds had lower temperature (28.9 vs. 31.2 °C), turbidity (31.6 vs. 40.9 NTU), dissolved oxygen (5.76 vs. 6.91 mg L−1), and ammonium, nitrite, phosphate, total nitrogen and total phosphorus concentrations. Shading explained 8.0% and 10.4% of bacterial and eukaryotic community variation respectively, while alpha diversity remained largely unchanged. Diatom relative abundance was higher under shading (46.2% vs. 26.7%), whereas dinoflagellate abundance was lower. From August to September, NPV communities shifted from diatom to ciliate dominance, with ciliate abundance increasing from 2.1% to 45.4% and Vibrio from 0.6% to 9.8%. In contrast, diatom abundance remained stable and Vibrio remained below 2.5% in PV ponds. Shaded communities exhibited lower dispersion and broader niche breadth, although assembly processes did not differ between treatments. Cross-kingdom networks contained fewer bacteria–eukaryote associations under shading, but cohesion and resampled network topology remained comparable. Overall, PV shading was associated with cooler, less nutrient-rich water and persistent diatom dominance; however, reduced dissolved oxygen highlights the importance of aeration management in fishery–PV systems.
