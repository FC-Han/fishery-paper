本周研究概览

本周共收录8篇渔业相关论文，研究方向涵盖渔业治理与政策（采购问责、鲑鱼养殖标签与追溯）、渔业生态学（温盐锋对饵料鱼营养生态的影响、鳟鱼捕获放流的能量代价）、水产养殖工程与微生物学（RAS生物滤器低温运行、鱼类加工废弃物堆肥）、水产病害快速检测（*Perkinsus olseni* RPA-PfAgo检测）以及海洋生物技术（深度学习辅助抗菌肽发现）。整体来看，本周论文以应用导向为主，治理法规类与病害检测类研究占比偏高，基础生态学研究相对有限；跨学科方法（深度学习、等温扩增）向水产领域的渗透是值得关注的趋势。本周无高影响力综合期刊论文发表。

The influence of accountability on procurement performance: a case study ministry of blue economy and fisheries Zanzibar
👤 Manzi, Abdillah; Sama, Hamisi 📅 2026-10-26
Accountability is a foundational pillar of procurement governance, yet quantitative evidence on how accountability mechanisms specifically shape procurement outcomes within individual government ministries in Zanzibar remains scarce. This study examined the influence of accountability on procurement performance at the Ministry of Blue Economy and Fisheries (MoBEF), Zanzibar. Guided by Institutional Theory and attached in a positivist research philosophy, study adopted a descriptive and explanatory quantitative design where a structured, five-point Likert-scale questionnaire was administered to 85 respondents drawn from a target population of 108 procurement officers, tender board members, finance officers, user department representatives, internal auditors, and registered suppliers, selected through stratified random sampling using Yamane’s (1967) formula. Data were analyzed descriptively and inferentially using IBM SPSS version 23, incorporating reliability testing, correlation, and multiple linear regressions. Descriptive results revealed that accountability practices were generally well established at MoBEF, with mean scores ranging from 3.90 to 4.12. Review and approval procedures and documentation practices attracted the strongest endorsement, while disciplinary enforcement for procurement irregularities recorded both the lowest mean (M = 3.90) and the greatest variability (SD = 0.89), indicating a structural enforcement gap within the Ministry’s accountability system. Multiple regression analysis confirmed that accountability significantly and positively predicted procurement performance (β = 0.27, SE = 0.07, t = 3.57, p = .001), alongside fairness (β = 0.31, p < .001) and transparency (β = 0.29, p = .002); the overall model was statistically significant (F(3, 81) = 28.45, p < .001, R² = 0.520). The null hypothesis was rejected. These findings confirm that while formal accountability structures are well established within MoBEF, inconsistent disciplinary enforcement limits their full effect on procurement performance. The study recommends strengthening audit follow-through, enforcing consistent sanctions for non-compliance, and institutionalizing structured monitoring systems.

Influence of a thermohaline front on the trophic ecology of the small pelagic fish Lycengraulis grossidens (Clupeiformes: Engraulidae)
👤 Marcilese, María Manuela; Parietti, Manuela; Temperoni, Bren... 📅 2026-10-26
Abstract Lycengraulis grossidens is economically important for artisanal fisheries and prey of top predators in the Río de la Plata (RP) (SW Atlantic). In particular, Samborombón Bay (SB)—RAMSAR site and key marine summer feeding area for the species—is strongly affected by climate variability. Diet composition, feeding strategy and dietary shifts related to fish size and to an existing thermohaline front between two SB zones were determined in late austral summer (April). A high incidence of brackish/marine prey was hypothesized, varying ontogenetically and in relation with the front. Trophic incidence exceeded 90%. Neomysis americana (Mysidacea) dominated the diet, followed by decapod larvae, gastropods and teleosts. The feeding strategy was specialist at the population level for N. americana but generalist for other prey. Both nMDS and GLM analyses supported the hypothesis, as N. americana , copepods and gastropods were more consumed in North SB, while teleosts South SB, linked to the thermohaline front affecting prey distribution. Smaller individuals (<100 mm) consumed copepods and decapod larvae, whereas larger individuals (>150 mm) increasingly fed on N. americana and gastropods. Unlike previous studies reporting piscivory at larger sizes, SB specimens maintained a zooplankton‐based diet, with a strong preference for N. americana . Results highlight the species' trophic plasticity and the influence of environmental and ontogenetic factors. This study provides valuable new information on the trophic ecology of L. grossidens in marine waters after a gap of over 60 years, contributing to a better understanding of its trophic role in highly dynamic estuarine and coastal ecosystems.

Energetic costs of catch and release angling in B rook T rout ( S alvelinus fontinalis ) at two temperatures
👤 Hodgson, Ryan Y.; Holder, Peter E.; Nowell, Liane B.; Cooke,... 📅 2026-10-26
Abstract Catch and release (C&R) angling is a common practice in recreational fisheries, which can involve a range of acute stressors for fish that lead to physiological and behavioural impairments. Following a C&R event, energy is expended to fuel recovery, while a cessation in feeding can limit energy intake. While the physiological and behavioural consequences of C&R have been extensively studied, the extent of the bioenergetic impacts has rarely been characterized. This study aimed to assess the energetic impacts of C&R stressors on Brook Trout ( Salvelinus fontinalis )—a cold‐water salmonid sensitive to temperature perturbations and air exposure. Adult Brook Trout were subjected to simulated angling events (i.e. 30s chase, 30s chase and 10s air exposure) or served as controls at 10°C and 15°C. Respirometry was used to measure energy expenditure through excess post‐exercise oxygen consumption (EPOC), while a feeding experiment determined the time to return to feeding. Energy expenditure and reductions in energy consumption were incorporated into a bioenergetics model to predict growth outcomes following captures (i.e. 1, 3, 5) over 30 days. The stressors simulated here resulted in a maximum growth deviation of 4.1% (95% CI [2.8–6.5]) after a single capture and 21.0% (95% CI [14.6–33.4]) after five captures. Across temperatures, Brook Trout experienced higher peak metabolic rates at 15°C, but rapid recovery resulted in similar energetic costs of recovery to 10°C. However, our bioenergetic simulations revealed that prolonged (24 h) elevated daily temperature (15°C) occupancy raised metabolic energy expenditure negating the benefits of a faster resumption of feeding. Nevertheless, the moderate simulated C&R stressors tested here within the optimal temperature range of Brook Trout resulted in relatively small energetic consequences, even with numerous simulated captures. Future research should explore the bioenergetic effects of more intense (but still reasonably common) C&R stressors, including longer air exposure periods, hooking injuries and temperature extremes.

Recombinase Polymerase Amplification Combined With the Programmable Nuclease Pf Ago for Rapid Detection of Perkinsus olseni in Shellfish
👤 Wang, Pei; Qin, Yuxin; Huang, Yidan; Ren, Yuxuan; Lin, Yan; ... 📅 2026-10-26
ABSTRACT Perkinsus olseni is a major pathogenic parasite that threatens shellfish aquaculture and can trigger mass mortality and considerable economic losses. Conventional culture‐based assays and PCR‐based detection protocols for P. olseni recommended by the World Organization for Animal Health (OIE) involve long assay durations and heavy dependence on sophisticated instruments, making them unsuitable for on‐site rapid screening. To establish a simple, rapid and highly sensitive detection method for early warning of P. olseni at aquaculture sites, this study integrated isothermal recombinase polymerase amplification (RPA) with the specific‐recognition capacity of Pf Ago nuclease to construct an RPA‐ Pf Ago detection assay. The assay achieved favourable specificity with a limit of detection as low as 30 copies. Its practical applicability was validated using 55 real‐world shellfish samples. Performing at constant temperatures, the RPA‐ Pf Ago detection assay features a simple procedure and short turnaround time, effectively addressing the drawbacks of conventional detection approaches. This assay is suitable for shellfish seed quarantine and routine pathogen surveillance in shellfish farms, and provides solid support for the early prevention and control of perkinsosis caused by P. olseni .

Deep-Learning-Assisted Discovery of Marine Antimicrobial Peptides: A Roadmap Integrating Omics Mining, Structure-Guided Diffusion Design, and Sustainable Aquaculture Translation
👤 Liu, Bo; Liu, Hanjing; Jia, Yunna; He, Jie; Wang, Ye; Jiang,... 📅 2026-10-26
Antimicrobial peptides (AMPs) offer diverse antibacterial mechanisms, but candidate generation alone does not resolve the linked demands of potency, host safety, stability, manufacturability, and application-specific exposure. This review presents a roadmap that integrates two complementary computational routes for marine AMP discovery. De novo generation expands sequence space, whereas omics mining preserves biological provenance and enables recovery of encoded or encrypted peptides from marine organisms and microbiomes. Structure-guided diffusion is positioned as a selective third route when a defined target, motif, or stable structural template is available, rather than as a universal replacement for sequence-based AMP models. We further examine aquaculture as a translational setting in which efficacy must be evaluated together with salt and feed tolerance, environmental fate, tissue residues, resistance risk, and export-related requirements. Current evidence remains limited at the direct intersection of marine provenance and deep learning, particularly for target-conditioned structural design. We therefore propose an evidence-based workflow that links marine sequence resources, model selection, matched experimental validation, and application-specific deployment. This framework is intended to move marine AMP research from candidate prediction toward reproducible and testable translation.

Long-Term Low- and Normal-Temperature Operation of Commercial Recirculating Aquaculture System Biofilters: Contrasting Microbial Communities and Predicted Nitrogen-Cycling Profiles
👤 Jiang, Wenwen; Fu, Qi; Liu, Tingting; Li, Li; Wang, Guodong;... 📅 2026-10-26
Microorganisms in biofilters are essential to recirculating aquaculture systems (RASs). Low temperature is an important environmental stress influencing the performance of the biofilter in RASs. However, how the microorganisms in the biofilters respond to low temperature remains unclear. In this study, we compared the microbial communities of two commercial RAS biofilters operating under low- and normal-temperature regimes across four seasons. Both biofilters generally kept low ammonia and nitrite concentrations, although the low-temperature biofilter exhibited higher nitrite levels overall with a more pronounced seasonal increase. The low-temperature biofilter also exhibited lower bacterial diversity, fewer detected taxa, and substantial shifts in community composition. The microbial communities were strongly associated with TOC, alkalinity, and inorganic nitrogen. Its co-occurrence network was smaller and more centralized, with greater clustering and shorter average path lengths, indicating highly concentrated microbial associations. Predicted nitrogen-cycle profiles also differed between the two biofilters. The predicted nitrification profile did not differ significantly, whereas the predicted denitrification profile was lower in the low-temperature biofilter. These findings indicate that the two commercial systems harbored distinctly different microbial communities, reflecting differences in specific processes. Collectively, mature RAS biofilters sustained ammonia and nitrite removal despite substantial community simplification, whereas individual predicted nitrogen-cycling processes exhibited distinct predicted patterns under the two operating regimes.
